# The New Inventors-20090729200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, broadcast on ABC1. The show features a panel of judges evaluating three distinct inventions submitted by inventors. The first invention is a bamboo de-brancher designed by an unnamed inventor, which attaches to a standard angle grinder to quickly and safely remove branches from bamboo stalks. The inventor explains that the tool provides a smooth, furniture-ready finish that is superior to traditional hand tools like machetes, which are labor-intensive and can damage the bamboo structure. The device features self-retracting blades for safety and is intended for use in farming, plantations, and zoos. The second invention is a compact, waterproof swag for homeless people, created by Jane Madden. The device functions as a discreet shoulder bag or backpack during the day, which can be rolled out at night to form a sleeping mat with a high-density foam mattress and canvas sheet. Madden explains that the design is simple and robust, costing approximately sixty dollars to make, and is produced by prisoners who donate their labor. The goal is to provide warmth, dryness, and dignity to individuals sleeping rough on the streets until they can secure permanent housing. The third invention is a truck suspension and road monitoring system called the Checkway Eliminator, developed by Roger Sack. The system uses sensors to monitor a truck's load distribution and suspension health while simultaneously using GPS to record the location of road defects such as potholes. This data is transmitted to road authorities for preventative maintenance, potentially reducing the need for static weigh-in stations. The judges deliberate on the need, originality, and design of the three inventions. Roger Sack and his Checkway Eliminator are declared the winner of the judges' choice for the episode, while viewers are invited to vote for the people's choice award. The program concludes with a brief news segment covering local crime and sports updates.
