## Summary

This recording appears to be a segment from a talk or lecture by Mark Pesce, focusing on organizational dynamics in the face of rapid technological or market shifts. The speaker discusses the challenges companies face when they are caught off guard by sudden transformations in their operating environment. He highlights a critical disconnect that arises between the organization, its customers, and its shareholders during these periods of uncertainty. Pesce argues that this disconnect creates a precarious situation for businesses, even those that believe they have been operating correctly. He describes the feeling of being "naked and exposed" as the world changes around them, leaving leaders unable to determine how to proceed. The core issue, he suggests, is not a lack of effort or understanding on the part of the organization, but rather the speed and nature of the external changes. Businesses that previously believed they had a solid grasp of their market and their own capabilities suddenly find themselves without a clear direction. The speaker emphasizes that most organizations are inherently resistant to change, preferring stability and established processes. However, the external environment forces a necessity for adaptation. Pesce notes that while organizations do not naturally want to change, they become "hungry for a change" when the status quo becomes untenable due to external pressures. This hunger is driven by the need to survive and remain relevant in a landscape where previous knowledge and strategies are no longer sufficient.
