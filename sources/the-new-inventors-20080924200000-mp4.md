# The New Inventors-20080924200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges consisting of agricultural scientist Chris Russell, interior designer Alison Page, and woodworker Richard Vaughan. The first invention presented is the RAPCO, a reusable, waterproof furniture cover with integrated handles designed to assist removalists in safely moving heavy items like sofas. The second invention is Aztecs, a perspex tool developed by Dr. Kim Miller from Melbourne that measures tactile sensitivity by allowing patients to run their fingers across graded ridges. The third invention is a ball retriever created by Jared Richards from Foster, Victoria, which uses a mirror and finger grips to pick up balls from roofs and gutters without requiring users to climb ladders. The judges evaluate the inventions based on originality, design, and market need. Chris Russell and Richard Vaughan vote for the Aztecs, citing its potential as a standard diagnostic and therapeutic tool for patients with nerve injuries or diabetes. Alison Page votes for the RAPCO, highlighting its environmental benefits and practical design for the removal industry. The Aztecs is declared the winner of the Inventor of the Year title for the episode. The program also includes a segment called Whatever Happened, which follows up on Sheldon King and his box seat, noting that the product is now installed in stadiums for two English soccer clubs. The broadcast concludes with a news summary covering the NRL, specifically Cameron Smith's injury and ban, the U.S. government's Wall Street bailout plan, and an auction for Sir Donald Bradman's cricket bat.
