# The New Inventors-20090701200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes agricultural scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and designer Sally Deminks. The first invention is a machine created by Tony Rockup from Far North Queensland that efficiently removes plastic mulch from agricultural fields. The device uses rattle bars to shake off dirt and debris, spooling the clean plastic into a bale for recycling. Rockup explains that the machine reduces the labor time from hours to about twenty minutes and costs $23,500. The judges discuss the environmental benefits of recycling the plastic and the machine's viability for farmers managing large acreages. The second invention is Tile Link, a roof safety anchor system designed by Arvo Poldma from Hawkesbury, north of Newcastle. This system allows workers to attach safety harnesses to a roof without cutting the waterproof sarking or using traditional lead flashing. It features a concertina of steel that stretches to cushion the impact of a fall, similar to a bungee jump. Poldma demonstrates the mechanism using a forklift and a weighted object, showing how the system absorbs energy gradually. The judges praise the design for its aesthetic integration and safety benefits, noting that it addresses the high rate of DIY-related deaths from roof falls. The final invention is a reusable, filtered water bottle called Three Two One, presented by an unnamed inventor. The bottle features a carbon filter that removes chlorine and heavy metals from tap water, aiming to reduce the environmental impact of single-use plastic bottles. The presenter highlights that the bottle is BPA-free and stylish, encouraging users to replace purchased bottled water with filtered tap water. The judges commend the design and ethical material choices, though they question the filter's lifespan and the bottle's capacity. The episode concludes with the judges voting for their favorite invention and viewers being invited to vote for the People's Choice award. The broadcast ends with a news segment covering local events, including a bikie gang brawl at Sydney Airport and the crash of a Yemeni airliner.
