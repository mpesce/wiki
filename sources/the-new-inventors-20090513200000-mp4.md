# The New Inventors-20090513200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The broadcast features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes agricultural scientist Chris Russell, engineering professor Veena Sahajwala, and futurist Mark Pesce. The first invention is the Water Boy, a dog water bowl designed by Michael Jones from Brisbane. Inspired by a personal loss, the two-section bowl allows dogs to tip it over repeatedly without spilling the entire reservoir, automatically refilling from a central plug. The second invention, presented by Phil Snowden, is a set of hay caps, which are covers for individual hay bales that protect against rain and wind while allowing the stack to breathe, reducing fire risk. The third invention is Down-to-Earth Lighting by Brad Wade, a magnetic wand system that allows users to change halogen light bulbs from ground level, eliminating the need for ladders. The judges debate the merits of the three submissions, weighing originality, design, and safety. Chris Russell and Mark Pesce ultimately select the hay caps as the winner of the night, citing their simplicity and significant safety benefits for farmers. Veena Sahajwala votes for the Water Boy, praising its clever design. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award, followed by promotional segments for other ABC programs such as Gardening Australia and the drama series Dirt Game. The transcript also includes brief mentions of previous winners and a closing quote from Jane Fonda.
