# 3-02 Socio-ability_ Reinventing the Mobile Phone.mp3

## Summary

Three-part talk: (1) social networks, (2) mobiles, (3) bringing the two strands together to see 'where the mobile phone is probably and probably very profitably going.' He starts with MySpace being sold to News Corp for over a billion AUD, but insists that's not really what a social network is: 'A social network is something much purer, much older, much cleaner than that. It comes from one basic fact — human beings are inherently social.' A lot of brain development is given over to modeling and understanding social interactions, and the social brain develops on different timelines for boys (peters off ~11-12) and girls (goes on intensively until ~16). He closes with a cautionary tale from South by Southwest: 300 people using Dodgeball in a swarm, and 'it became impossible to figure out which messages were meaningful and which were not' — they created a social network but 'aren't actually using the social network they've created to figure out which messages should have priority... they've gathered all this data, and then they're ignoring it.' He also mentions his own early phone app, Live Record, for feeding your social network your likes and dislikes.
