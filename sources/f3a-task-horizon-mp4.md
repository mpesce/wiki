## Summary

Clip on the 'task horizon' — how long an agent can go before it falls over. METR (METR.org) has been tracking it: ~1 minute in 2023, then a regular ~7-month doubling cadence similar to Moore's law. In a three-week period in November of last year, all three frontier labs shipped major releases (Gemini 3.1, Opus 4.6, GPT 5.2), and at that point the task horizon surpassed a key threshold.
