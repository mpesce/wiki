# The New Inventors-20071128200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is the grand final of the Australian television program New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The episode features the top five inventions selected from 120 entries submitted during the year, with judges Chris Russell, Bernie Hobbs, and Sally Deminks evaluating the finalists for the Invention of the Year award. The show also presents the People's Choice Award and the Les Ismore Award, the latter presented by Young Australian of the Year Tanya Major. The five finalists presented are Kevin Inkster from Perth with the All-Saw, a chainsaw alternative that uses an elliptical blade motion to reduce kickback and allow cutting through earth; Valerie Gay and Peter Leydeckers from Sydney with a personal health monitor that uses mobile phone software to detect cardiac arrest and alert emergency services; Glenn Thompson from Orange with the Thompson Coupling, a constant velocity joint with no load-bearing sliding surfaces; Don Morgan from Brisbane with the Conehead helmet liner, which uses deceleration cones to improve head protection; and Anthony Dixon from Sydney with the Water Fresh process, a mechanical sonic device that smashes bacteria to disinfect water. The judges deliberate in a separate room and select a unanimous winner. The 2007 Invention of the Year is awarded to Don Morgan for the Conehead helmet liner. The winner receives a package of prizes from Australia, Oz Industry, the CSIRO, and IP Australia to assist in developing the invention. The show also highlights the success of previous winners, such as Chris Bosch, whose air compressor invention has expanded to 27 countries. The broadcast concludes with promotional segments for ABC Shop and upcoming television programs.
