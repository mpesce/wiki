# 1-01 The Future of Me.mp3

## Summary

This recording is a talk titled The Future of Me, delivered by a speaker known as Fluffy, who identifies as the event producer for the Speaker's Corner at the Oracle at Arrowhead. The event, scheduled for Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday from three to five p.m., features discussions on the future of the self, society, and the world. Fluffy introduces the day's program, which includes talks by Ruth and Hangu on the poetry of Wallace Stevens and a closing session by Zid and Function Arrowhead on the brain and the future. The main body of the talk addresses the human consciousness of death and the various religious and philosophical responses to mortality, ranging from Christian salvation to Buddhist Bardo realms. Fluffy discusses the transformative power of knowing one’s expiration date, citing Elisabeth Kübler-Ross’s five stages of grief and the case of a man in Ghana who knew the day of his death. Fluffy reveals that he learned his own date of death, June 19, 2009, over a decade prior through shamanic practice, leaving him with less than three years to live. He explains that this knowledge has fundamentally altered his life, eliminating long-term planning and retirement savings in favor of an urgent focus on immediate action and love. Fluffy recounts witnessing the death of a close friend, David, in 1993, which provided him with empirical evidence of the soul’s departure and led to his core belief that only love matters. This experience, combined with his knowledge of his own death, drives his current mission to share truth and love. He concludes by stating that even if his prediction is wrong, the loss is minimal compared to the clarity and urgency gained from living with the certainty of his own end. The talk ends with a brief pause before the next segment of the event.
