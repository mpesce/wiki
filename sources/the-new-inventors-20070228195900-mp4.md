# The New Inventors-20070228195900.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by Adam Hills. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges consisting of agricultural scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and interior designer Allison Page. The episode begins with a brief tribute to the late Billy Thorpe and a preview of upcoming ABC programming, including a Kylie Minogue tour and a segment on Jacinta Thurston. The first invention presented is the Move Ya Bastard, created by Carl Goodwin from Adelaide. This battery-driven device attaches to a car wheel to move disabled vehicles around workshops, solving the back-breaking task of manually pushing cars. The judges discuss its originality and practicality for mechanics, noting that it allows for safe maneuvering from the driver's seat. The second invention is the Go4Tool buggy, designed by Jacinta Thurston from Forbes to help her mother with gardening. Made from a recycled golf buggy, it serves as a tool carrier and includes a fold-up potting table to reduce bending. The judges praise its modular design and the ingenuity of using a sewing machine and welding to create it. The third invention is CuffSafe, developed by former police officer Jason Owen from Terrigal. This floor-mounted unit secures handcuffs, allowing officers to unlock restraints without looking away from the offender, thereby reducing the risk of injury during fingerprinting or search procedures. After evaluating the inventions on criteria such as design, originality, marketability, and need, the judges unanimously select the Move Ya Bastard as the winner of the night. Carl Goodwin is announced as the winner and enters the running for the Inventor of the Year award. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award and a preview of the next episode of Spics and Specs.
