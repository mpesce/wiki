# The New Inventors-20090923200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is a segment from the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes futurist and author Mark Pesce, surgeon Professor Fiona Wood, and designer Sally Deminks. The first invention presented is the Fast Track, a roof rack system by Cameron Oldfield that uses internal straps and a cam mechanism to secure loads, eliminating the need for external ropes or ratchet straps. The second invention involves hydrogel scaffolds developed by Cameron Ferris, which utilize gelatin gum and conducting polymers to mimic soft tissue and potentially grow replacement organs or repair damaged tissue. The third invention is a full-length door hinge designed by David Ashard that eliminates the gap between the door and frame to prevent finger crush injuries, a problem that affects millions of people annually. The judges discuss the merits of each invention, noting the convenience of the roof rack, the long-term medical potential of the hydrogel, and the immediate safety impact of the door hinge. Mark Pesce specifically highlights the importance of the door hinge in preventing amputations, particularly for children, and suggests its application to car doors. After deliberation, the panel selects David Ashard and his finger-saving hinge as the Inventor of the Year, citing its clear path to implementation and tangible safety benefits. The segment concludes with a prompt for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award. The recording ends with a transition to a news broadcast covering a dust storm in New South Wales, the relocation of a pedophile, climate change discussions in New York, and Australian military action against Somali pirates.
