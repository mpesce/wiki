# 3-01 The Audio Tour.mp3

## Summary

This recording captures a presentation by artist Peggy Nelson at the third and final day of the Speakers Corner of the Oracle at Arrowhead, an event associated with the Burning Man festival. Introduced by Mark Pesce, who notes he helped bring Nelson to the event in 1999 as the set designer for a DJ Christ Superstar performance, Nelson shares selections from an audio tour installation she is creating for the year. The talk is structured as a series of dream reports and personal anecdotes that explore themes of existentialism, community, and the arbitrary nature of meaning. Nelson begins by describing a dream where the festival has expanded to encompass "Regular Man," comparing the crowd to tadpoles or serotonin in a synapse. She then discusses observing Terracotta Warriors inside a hollowed-out shell near a domed structure, which she describes as a Baroque monstrosity of excess. This observation triggers a sense of existential panic, which she ultimately accepts as an accurate response to a life without intrinsic teleology or fate. She argues that the feeling of free fall is valid because meaning is extrinsic and temporary, and that comfort in established beliefs is dangerous. The presentation shifts to a personal story about a former boyfriend and their decision to join the Communist Party, which she describes as a beautiful theory that failed to match reality. She recounts their activities, such as organizing speakers and vegetarian potlucks, noting that the revolution did not go as planned. She then introduces the sport of bog snorkeling as a metaphor for enlightenment, describing the struggle to navigate through thick mud with sheer willpower and insistent belief.
