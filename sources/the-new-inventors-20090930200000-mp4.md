# The New Inventors-20090930200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes agricultural scientist Chris Russell, surgeon and inventor Professor Fiona Wood, and designer Sally Deminks. The first invention is a portable hydraulic power pack designed by Tony Henry, which attaches to a trailer and can be towed by a utility vehicle. This device allows farmers to power various implements, such as hay feeders and grain augers, remotely without the need for a full-sized tractor, offering a cost-effective alternative to purchasing expensive heavy machinery. The second invention, presented by Max Cherry and Raymond Ng from Melbourne, is a fingerprint scanning interface called Raymax. This device retrofits onto standard door locks, allowing users to replace physical keys with their fingerprints. The inventors demonstrate the installation process and the security features, noting that the system can store up to 100 fingerprints and is powered by AA batteries. The judges discuss the practicality of the system, including its sensitivity to wet or dirty fingers and its durability against environmental factors. The final invention is an internal bore casing cutter created by Chris Davidson, a farmer from Chinchilla, Queensland. This tool is designed to cut PVC pipes left in the ground by mining companies from the inside out, eliminating the need to dig large holes to remove the casing. Davidson explains that his method reduces soil disturbance and allows for easier property restoration. The judges debate the merits of the three inventions, with Russell favoring the casing cutter for its necessity, Deminks appreciating the design of the power pack, and Wood highlighting the accessibility of the fingerprint lock. Ultimately, the panel selects Chris Davidson's internal bore casing cutter as the winner of the night, placing him in the running for the Inventor of the Year award. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote for their favorite invention for the People's Choice Award.
