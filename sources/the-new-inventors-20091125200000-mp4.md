# The New Inventors-20091125200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is the broadcast of the 2009 New Inventors Grand Final, an Australian television program hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show celebrates new Australian inventions, narrowing down 40 episode winners to five grand finalists. The judges for the night are agricultural scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and designer Sally Deminks. The program features several award categories, including the Young Inventor of the Year, the Les Ismore Award for social good, the People's Choice Award, and the main Invention of the Year. The five grand finalists presented are Colin Chamberlain from Mackay, who designed a safety propeller with rounded edges to prevent injury to humans and marine life; Matthew Walker from Brisbane, who created a visual security system that overlays a card pattern on a screen to generate unique passwords; Bob Holland and Paul Brockwell, who developed intelligent plastic sensors that change color to indicate food freshness; Micah, a portable medical laboratory device smaller than a beer can for diagnosing diseases in remote areas; and David Teale and Madhu Neeley, who invented circuit boards sealed in plastic for waterproofing and easier recycling. The broadcast includes segments with previous winners, such as Dean Cameron, who presents the BrightSpark Award to Austin Teikle for a motorcycle safety vest, and Gabby Hollows, who presents the Les Ismore Award to Dr. Stephanie Watson and Dr. Nick Guilamo for a corneal repair technique. The People's Choice Award is won by Kenneth Miles for the Vaxac vaccine cooler. Dick Smith returns to present the Invention of the Year award. After deliberation, the judges select Colin Chamberlain and his safety propeller as the 2009 winner, citing its simplicity and life-saving potential. The show concludes with a look at the winners' future plans and a preview of upcoming ABC programming.
