## Summary

This recording is a transcript from an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges comprising engineer James Bradford Moody, engineering professor Veena Sahajwala, and journalist Christine Knimmel. The episode begins with a brief news segment discussing government priorities regarding unemployment benefits and jobs, followed by promotional clips for other ABC1 programs. The first inventor, Perry Bennett, presents Benox blocks, a lightweight, interlocking building material designed to replace traditional bricks. He explains that the blocks are hollow, fire-resistant, and use a special adhesive instead of mortar for most layers, allowing for faster construction. They debate the merits of each, with the Aqua Curve praised for its environmental impact and the Benox blocks noted for their innovation in construction materials. Ultimately, the panel selects Perry Bennett and his Benox blocks as the winner of the night.
