# The New Inventors-20070516200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by Alison Page. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, including James Moody and Steve. The first inventor, Peter, demonstrates the Keyless Lifestyle Controller, a miniature RFID tag that attaches to a watch band to unlock both house doors and car doors. He explains that the system costs approximately $1,200 for a home installation and $400 to $500 for a vehicle, with replacement tags costing $11. Peter notes that the technology is durable, having been worn for two years, and addresses security concerns by stating that the encryption makes scanning the tag difficult. The second inventor, Steve McNeil, presents the Adjustable Link Strap, a rubber strap designed to replace traditional ratchet or Ocky straps for securing loads to roof racks. He highlights that traditional straps can cause severe eye injuries if they snap back, whereas his design uses a double arrowhead mechanism that prevents the strap from coming undone once attached. The strap is sold in one-meter lengths for around $10 and can be joined for unlimited length. The panel discusses the material, noting that while rubber is susceptible to sun damage, the inventors are exploring polyurethane and other chemical-proof materials. The third inventor, Catherine Constable, introduces the Operator-Adjusted Mirror for large mining dump trucks. The device allows drivers to adjust their own side mirrors using a T-bar handle without tools, eliminating the need to wait for a fitter and reducing idle time costs. The mirror is constructed from stainless steel, nylon, and Teflon to withstand the corrosive water used in mine washing. It costs between $1,580 and $1,780 plus GST and is designed to last three times longer than standard mirrors. The judges ultimately select Steve McNeil and the Adjustable Link Strap as the winner of the night, citing its high safety factor and market need. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the People’s Choice Award and a quote from Albert Einstein.
