# The New Inventors-20070530200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is a special episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin, focusing on water scarcity and conservation. The show features a panel of analysts including agricultural scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and inventor Sally Deminks, who discuss the severity of the water crisis in Australia. The episode highlights the high per capita water usage in the country compared to its status as the driest continent, noting that freshwater supplies in cities like Canberra and Ballarat have dropped significantly. The panelists share their personal water usage audits, revealing that even those conscious of conservation use more water than expected. The main segment features Michael Mobs, a resident of inner Sydney, who demonstrates a fully self-sufficient water system in his terrace house. He explains how rainwater is collected in a 10,000-litre tank for indoor use, while grey water is filtered and reused for toilets, laundry, and gardens. Mobs notes that he has disconnected from the main water supply and sewer, saving approximately 100,000 litres of water annually. The discussion explores the scalability of such systems to apartments and addresses barriers like cost and government regulation. The program also revisits previous inventions, including the Every Drop Shower Saver and the Enviro Save water system, discussing their market challenges and regulatory hurdles. Inventors Micho Sullivan and John Grimes return to discuss their modular rainwater tanks and active absorption filtration systems, respectively, noting recent sales growth following accreditation. The episode concludes with a presentation by Dr. Max Whisson on a wind-powered device that extracts water from the air using refrigeration panels, capable of producing thousands of litres daily. The host announces the winner of the water diary challenge and ends with a quote from the Roman playwright Titus Plautus.
