# ExpandingMind010710.mp3

## Summary

Episode of 'Expanding Mind' (Progressive Radio Network, 1 July 2010), hosted by Eric Davis with co-host Maja Intoh, featuring Mark Pesce. The show explores 'the culture of consciousness.' Mark reflects on discovering MUDs (multi-user dimensions/dungeons) in the early 1990s — 'like EverQuest or World of Warcraft, except it was all writing': people described the places, their actions, and their dialogue in real time. What grew out of it was 'an amazing interactive real-time collaborative writing project that felt very much like you were actually living and moving through this half-imagined space.' The takeaway: with the exploding digital universe, 'the opportunities for kinds of collaboration go beyond even the sense of that term' — 'what we're getting towards is something where our minds, our spirits, our impulses, our sense of ourselves is being challenged, transformed, opened up as we weave ourselves together.' The social networks and Twitter of the last few years are 'signs of some other even deeper ways in which technology and media are changing our sense of selves and the way we connect with each other.'
