# The New Inventors-20090318200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, including science journalist Bernie Hobbs, designer Sally Deminks, and agricultural scientist Chris Russell. The first invention presented is an all-in-one water pump, collection hose, storage unit, carry frame, and filtration device designed by Liam O'Brien. This system is intended for developing nations, allowing users to pump water from difficult sources, carry it ergonomically on their back, and filter it through activated carbon to remove biological contaminants. The device is designed to be manufactured locally for under twenty dollars, addressing the significant physical burden and health risks associated with collecting dirty water. The second invention is a self-supporting dome shell housing system developed by Chris Brown and John Currie. The structure is built using an inflatable balloon form into which layers of glass fiber reinforced concrete and polyurethane foam are sprayed. This creates a highly weather-resistant, energy-efficient building that can withstand category five cyclones and does not require internal beams or columns. The judges discussed the structural integrity and thermal properties of the dome, noting its potential for use in remote areas or as a fire shelter, while also questioning the long-term environmental impact of the materials used. The final invention is the Rio Jack, a simple lever tool created by Michael Crow to assist construction workers in placing bar chairs under reinforcement mesh before pouring concrete. This device eliminates the need for workers to squat and lift heavy mesh, thereby reducing the risk of back injury. The tool is priced at thirty dollars and was praised by the judges for its simplicity, safety, and effectiveness in solving a common occupational health problem. The judges ultimately selected the Rio Jack as the winner of the episode, citing its clear design and practical utility. The program concludes with a quote from Alexander Graham Bell and a preview of upcoming content.
