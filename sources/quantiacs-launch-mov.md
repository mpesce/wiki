## Summary

This recording is a brief promotional introduction or pitch delivered by Martin, the CEO and founder of Quantiax. The main topic is the company's business model, which centers on creating the first marketplace for trading algorithms. Martin explains that Quantiax connects user-generated quantitative trading strategies with capital from institutional investors. He details the revenue structure, noting that the company handles the trading operations for these investors. Under this arrangement, institutional investors retain eighty percent of the profits, while Quantiax charges a twenty percent fee. This twenty percent fee is then split equally between Quantiax and the quant developer who created the specific trading strategy. Martin further elaborates on the two primary sides of the marketplace. On one side are smart developers who build trading algorithms, a group he describes as potentially including students, professors, or any individual with the necessary skills. On the other side are institutional investors who utilize these strategies to trade. The core value proposition presented is that developers can earn a portion of the profits from their algorithms, while investors gain access to a variety of user-generated quantitative strategies. No specific date is mentioned in the transcript, and no other speakers are named. The entire content focuses on defining the company's role as a bridge between algorithm creators and capital providers in the financial trading sector.
