# Sunday.rm

## Summary

Final session of the weekend. Each of the three opens with 45 seconds to a minute on where they've landed. Mark recounts a moment from a yoga/chakra-and-tones exercise and tells a cautionary story: a healer 'who seemed to make light from his hands,' whom he brought to Rupert Sheldrake, William Bloom, and others who all pronounced him 'an awakened master... the real energy' — until it turned out the 'light' was a flashlight and the man was screwing everyone, getting 14-year-old girls pregnant, and didn't believe any of it himself. The lesson: 'not only can all these great minds be fooled, but that none of this [is reliable on its own].' The group then discusses responsibility — a participant's confrontation with the World Bank over a draft urban-transport policy that ignored disabled people (forcing 'community organizer mode'), and the 'good cops / bad cops' dynamics of activism. They circle the question of how much of the esoteric (vibrations, energies, lucid dreaming, the theosophical koshas or 'skins' of the yogic body, prana linked to electromagnetism) can be acknowledged by science. Mark closes with a Tello/Druid triad — 'know thy power, know thy wisdom, know thy time' — and notes 'our time is up.'
