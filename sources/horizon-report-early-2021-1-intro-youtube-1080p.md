# Horizon Report Early 2021 - 1 Intro YouTube 1080p.mp4

## Summary

In this short audio announcement, Mark Pesce introduces a new series of publications titled the Horizon Reports, which he began creating in mid-2020 during the rapid changes of the pandemic. Pesce explains that the reports were designed to provide insight into the future when traditional views were obscured by the crisis. He states that the first of what he expects to be three reports will be released at the beginning of 2021. The specific theme for this early 2021 report is the gray area, describing the transitional period where the world is emerging from the pandemic into a new, unfamiliar reality. Pesce characterizes this time as a shadow zone or threshold between ongoing pandemic conditions in some parts of the world and recovery in others, noting that most people are fumbling through this uncharted territory. He predicts that 2021 will feel long because it will involve significant learning and activity within this ambiguous space. Pesce outlines the distribution plan for the report, stating that the findings will be released weekly, specifically every Wednesday in Sydney time. The series is structured to break down the analysis over four weeks. The final installment in the fourth week will present a summary of findings categorized into known knowns, known unknowns, unknown knowns, and unknown unknowns. The ultimate goal of this framework is to help the audience plan effectively and make the most of their lives during the early part of 2021 while navigating the uncertainties of the gray area. The recording serves as a promotional introduction to this upcoming content series, emphasizing the need for structured guidance during a period of global transition.
