# The New Inventors-20070314195900.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James A Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes engineer James Bradfield Moody, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and returning judge Nick Morris. The first invention presented is the lullabub, an automatic cot rocker invented by Christopher Mitchell from Mermaid Beach. Mitchell explains that he created the device after suffering from exhaustion and postnatal depression while caring for his children. The lullabub uses four spring-based pods under the cot legs to create a gentle, rhythmic rocking motion for half an hour, simulating movements like a heartbeat or a car ride. It is designed to fit most cots, costs 249 dollars, and has sold nearly one thousand units. The second invention is the safe break, a gate post insulator and clip for electric fence gateways designed by a dairy farmer named Rob. The device is high-visibility and releases automatically upon impact, requiring 6.5 kilograms of pressure to disengage. This prevents damage to animals, vehicles, or the fence itself when they crash into the gate. The inventor notes that the device is durable, having been tested for up to 2,000 releases, and costs between nine and ten dollars. The third invention is the GEM solution, a system for filling expansion joints in brick walls, presented by Joe Borg. This system uses a casing, an expansion tie, and a cover strap to hide gaps and allow for structural movement without cracking. It is designed to save builders time and provide a neater finish, with a life expectancy of about 25 years. The judges deliberate on the merits of each invention, considering criteria such as originality, design, marketability, and need. Nick Morris initially votes for the lullabub, James Bradfield Moody votes for the GEM solution, and Bernie Hobbs votes for the safe break. After further discussion, the judges reach a consensus, with the lullabub winning the episode due to its strong market appeal, design, and ability to solve a significant problem for new parents.
