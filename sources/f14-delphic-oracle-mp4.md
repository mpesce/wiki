# F14_delphic-oracle.mp4

## Summary

Clip on trust in post-frontier ('mythos-class') systems that are smarter than us. Two approaches are coming: (1) we will only trust outputs that are formally provable — these systems will have to produce mathematical proofs of what they are saying before we can trust it; and (2) we go all the way back to an oracle-like / Delphic mode (like the symbol of Kumai).
