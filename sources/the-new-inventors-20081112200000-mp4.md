# The New Inventors-20081112200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin on ABC1. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes engineer James Bradfield Moody, designer Alison Page, and futurist Mark Pesce. The episode begins with a preview of upcoming ABC programming, including a documentary on the Aztec Empire and a segment on whale migration, before transitioning to the main competition. The first invention presented is an electronic nose designed to detect the smell of spray paint in real time to combat graffiti. The inventors, Bryn Hibbert and Graham Bell, demonstrate the device's ability to identify specific solvents from a distance of 45 meters, triggering an alert to security. They discuss the technology's potential applications in medical diagnostics and its cost-effectiveness compared to traditional cleanup methods. The second invention is the Bio Cube, a compact biodiesel processing unit developed by Sandy Kelly. This machine extracts oil from seeds like coconut and canola to produce up to 2,000 liters of biodiesel daily, intended for use in developing countries to provide local energy independence. Kelly explains the unit's safety, ease of use, and economic viability, noting that it can pay for itself in approximately eight months. The final invention is the Track Chair, a manual wheelchair modification by Alan and Adrian Beddwell that allows users to propel and steer the chair with one hand. This design frees the user's other hand for daily tasks such as carrying items or answering a phone. The inventors demonstrate the chair's efficiency, including its ability to climb ramps and maintain momentum while turning. The judges deliberate on the merits of each invention, with Mark Pesce praising the Track Chair's elegant design and the Bio Cube's cohesive engineering. Ultimately, the panel unanimously selects the Track Chair as the winner of the night, citing its strong marketability and the significant improvement it offers to the quality of life for wheelchair users. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote in the People's Choice Award and a brief preview of the comedy show Spicks and Specs.
