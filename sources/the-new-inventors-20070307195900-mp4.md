# The New Inventors-20070307195900.mp4

## Summary

This recording is a broadcast of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James A Lachlan. The episode features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges consisting of agricultural scientist Chris Russell, journalist and inventor Christine Kninnemont, and interior designer Alison Page. The first invention is a cargo cage for Ute tailgates, designed by Andrew and John, which allows users to carry over-length loads securely without drilling into the vehicle. The second invention, presented by Charles Shooter, is a valve safety lockout device for industrial plants, such as sugar refineries, which mechanically locks valves in a closed position to prevent accidental opening during maintenance. The third invention is the Wee Target, a toilet training aid for boys that uses heat-sensitive ink to reveal a surprise picture when hit by warm urine, encouraging concentration. The panel discusses the merits of each invention, focusing on originality, marketability, and necessity. While the Wee Target is praised for its fun factor and the cargo cage for its design, the panel largely agrees that the valve safety lockout offers the most significant safety improvements and broad industrial application. After a period of deliberation and a three-way tie, the judges reach a consensus to award the win to Charles Shooter for the valve safety lockout. The segment concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the people's choice award via text or online. The broadcast ends with promotional trailers for upcoming ABC programs, including Constructing Australia, Airplay, and Come Around the World in 80 Treasures, as well as a brief news segment regarding tsunami risks.
