# The New Inventors-20081126200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is the 2008 Grand Final of the Australian television program New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features a panel of judges, including agricultural expert Chris Russell and designer Alison Page, who select the top five inventions from forty finalists. The episode highlights several innovative technologies, beginning with Join Locks by Dean Cameron, a mechanical fastening system inspired by muscle tissue that allows for strong, flat-packable joints in construction and automotive applications. The second finalist is LumiFlex, a flexible, low-energy electroluminescent lighting technology developed by Gabriella and Ernest Kivaya, which offers a lightweight alternative to traditional lighting for displays and architectural features. The third invention is the Smart Hook, created by Hans Jusset, a biodegradable shield for longline fishing hooks designed to prevent the bycatch of seabirds and sea turtles. The fourth finalist is Quiet Wave, an acoustic insulation material developed by Philippe Donner that uses food residue to deflect sound vibrations, offering a cheaper and lighter solution for soundproofing and fire rating. The fifth invention is BioSol, a chemical additive by Ross Chandler that puts bacteria in sewerage pipes into hibernation to prevent pipe corrosion and reduce methane emissions. The show also presents the Les Ismore Award for social impact, won by Graham Tyson for a motorized straw drink dispenser, and the Bright Spark Award for young inventors, won by Rachel Latimer for the Easy Girth, a pulley system for tightening horse saddles. The People's Choice Award is given to Leonie West for the Adjustable Quilting Rule. After deliberation, the judges unanimously select Join Locks as the 2008 Invention of the Year. The segment concludes with a comedic performance by Rolf Harris, the inventor of the wobble board, who sings a song about kangaroos and cockatoos. The transcript includes various comedic interludes and judge banter, such as discussions about slowing down the planet to lengthen the day and the origins of the wobble board.
