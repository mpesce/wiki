# The New Inventors-20090204200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is the first episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors for the year 2009, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes agricultural scientist Chris Russell, designer Alison Page, and futurist Mark Pesci. The episode begins with a brief news segment from ABC 7:30, followed by the main program introduction. The first invention presented is the Road Transformer by Roger Chapman, a device made from recycled railway steel designed to smooth bumpy dirt roads in remote areas. Chapman explains that the tool is attached to a vehicle and dragged along the road to fill ruts and flatten corrugations, serving as a maintenance measure between full grading sessions. The judges discuss its potential for use in Aboriginal communities and the developing world, noting its low cost and simple design. The second invention is the Banana Blankie by Robert Watkins, a second-generation banana farmer from near Cairns. Watkins introduces a new packaging system that replaces the traditional multi-layer cartons with a single, breathable compartment. This design reduces the number of times a banana is handled from fifteen to two, significantly lowering damage rates and allowing the fruit to ripen naturally without gas treatment. The judges praise the innovation for its simplicity, cost-effectiveness, and potential application to other fruits like grapes and mangoes. The final invention is the Mighty Lift by Bill Grant from Morong, Victoria. This is a portable, manually operated lifting device that combines a ladder with a cable and winch system to transport heavy loads, such as air conditioning units, up to a roof. Grant demonstrates the assembly and operation of the device, highlighting its adjustability for different roof pitches and its safety features, including a braking system. The judges commend the invention for making a dangerous task safer and more efficient. At the conclusion of the episode, the judges vote on the best invention of the night. Mark Pesci, Chris Russell, and Alison Page all select the Banana Blankie as the winner, citing its ability to resolve multiple industry issues through a simple and elegant design.
