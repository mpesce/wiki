# The Bell can not be unrung.mp4

## Summary

This recording is a segment from an interview or discussion panel featuring Mark Pesce, who reflects on the impact of the pandemic on the workplace. The conversation centers on the concept of the future of work, specifically examining how organizations adapted to remote operations during the early stages of the global health crisis. Pesce recalls the specific date of March 25, 2020, when widespread lockdowns forced employees to work from home. He notes that there was an initial fear that business operations would collapse due to the sudden shift, but in reality, companies managed to continue functioning with minimal disruption. Pesce acknowledges the common fatigue associated with video conferencing tools like Zoom but emphasizes that the continuity of work revealed a previously unrecognized capacity for transformation within organizations, their people, and their technology. He argues that these resources for change were always present but only became necessary and accessible under the pressure of the crisis. A key point in his argument is that this shift is irreversible; he uses the metaphor that the bell cannot be unrung, meaning the lessons learned and the new ways of working established during that period will permanently alter the landscape of employment. The segment concludes with Pesce expressing his intent to further explore how society and businesses will integrate these pandemic-era lessons into their long-term strategies for the future of work.
