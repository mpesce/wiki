# hashrate.mkv

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the podcast Hashrate, hosted by Mark Jeffrey, featuring an interview with Mark Pesce. The two discuss the intersection of cryptocurrency regulation and international financial bodies. Pesce, a former engineer and media commentator, details his role as the chair of the V20, a virtual asset service provider summit associated with the G20. The conversation covers the history and mechanics of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) and the Financial Stability Board (FSB), explaining how these organizations issue recommendations that become national laws to combat money laundering and terrorist financing. Pesce outlines the timeline of crypto regulation, noting that the FATF published its initial recommendations for virtual assets in May 2019, which led to the development of the IVMS 01 protocol for data exchange between exchanges. He describes the 2019 V20 summit in Osaka, where exchange representatives met with regulators to workshop compliance solutions, and the 2022 summit in Bali, which coincided with the collapse of FTX. This event shifted the focus from anti-money laundering to prudential regulation, aiming to prevent financial contagion and ensure exchanges maintain sufficient reserves for customer deposits. The discussion also addresses the regulatory challenges posed by decentralized finance (DeFi). Pesce notes that regulators are struggling to apply traditional frameworks to permissionless protocols, with some jurisdictions considering requiring DeFi protocols to have registered directors to establish accountability. He predicts a future where DeFi may split into compliant, KYC-integrated systems and more anonymous "wild" DeFi. The interview concludes with Pesce mentioning that the International Organization of Securities Regulators (IOSCO) is likely the next body to address securities aspects of crypto, and he expresses his goal of making the new financial tools safer for all participants.
