# DIGIMART -When The Going Gets Weird.mp3

## Summary

Talk at DIGIMART (Mark had been at the National Film School for two years). He opens with the story of 'This Land' — a four-minute 2004 flash animation by the small internet company JibJack that used Woody Guthrie's song to satirize the Bush vs. Kerry election. JibJack seeded it to a few thousand people on their mailing list with the strategy 'if you post it, they will come,' hoping a million might see it; within three weeks it blew up. He then tells the story of RED vs. BLUE: fans making Halo machinima who 'don't own the copyright to Halo — Microsoft does,' and who expected the hammer to come down. Instead, Microsoft called and PAID them to make a Red vs. Blue episode to promote the Windows Developers Conference — having figured out that Red vs. Blue's popularity did not dilute the Halo franchise, and even put features into Halo 2 to make it easier for them to make games. The key takeaway: 'The more something is shared, the more valuable it becomes. It strikes against common sense... and different economics apply. As I stated in the title, the going's gotten weird. And when the going gets weird, the WEIRD turn pro.'
