# BecomingTranshuman.mp3

## Summary

A poetic/lyrical piece (likely an audio or spoken-word work) on the theme of transhumanism, structured as a meditation on the universe's origins and humanity's place in it. It opens cosmogonically — 'The universe begins not in light as we have been taught, but in song: a string 10^15 billion meters in length, turned upon itself as a cosmic loop, struck to sound an enunciation, a birth.' It traces the universe expanding and differentiating (gravity dropping away, the nuclear and electromagnetic forces separating, the strong and weak forces coming into discrete quantities — 1 2 3 5 8 13 21, the Fibonacci sequence — 'flowing back and forth between the crowded parts of the universe'). It moves through the age of giants (massive stars of protons and helium that burn brightly and briefly, then collapse, seeding the universe with hydrogen, helium, carbon, oxygen, nitrogen, and the heavier elements), and then to the human condition: the 'unbearable light,' the temptation of 'mind control satellites... a simulation of God,' and the choice of 'what path to the angels.' It closes with a vow — to accept 'the horror of the situation, the terror of the terminus' without flinching, 'without cyborgs or demons, angels or eschaton, but if it is just us, only us, together in the place where words cannot reach, what then is there that divides you from me? ... I lay me down to sleep and dreams come in which I must awaken you.'
