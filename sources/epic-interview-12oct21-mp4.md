# EPIC Interview 12Oct21.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an interview segment from a live event titled Resilient Live, featuring host Sally and guest Mark Pesce, a media and technology commentator. The conversation centers on the nature of personal resilience, with Pesce defining it as the deliberate choice to lean into pain and challenge rather than seeking the easy path. He cites advice from an elite rower friend, noting that life consists of countless small decisions, and consistently choosing the harder option leads to greater fulfillment and growth. Pesce emphasizes that resilience is not merely about enduring hardship but about maintaining a growth mindset where setbacks are viewed as temporary and opportunities for learning. The discussion shifts to the role of physical health in sustaining mental sharpness and creative output. Pesce explains that disciplined exercise, including yoga, provides the energy necessary for his prolific work in podcasts, writing, and technology commentary. He describes a counterintuitive process where allowing himself moments of boredom actually facilitates his best ideas, requiring a balance between disciplined activity and slowing down to let insights emerge. He frames his current life stage as the "third turn," a period focused on leveraging past achievements to leave a meaningful legacy, particularly by advocating for the transition to a sustainable, energy-abundant economy. Finally, the interview addresses the impact of pandemic lockdowns on social connection and resilience. Pesce shares how he maintained relationships during Sydney’s strict restrictions by meeting friends for walks and bike rides within permitted limits, highlighting how scarcity made these human connections more treasured. He argues that while virtual tools have become more integrated into work and friendship, the pandemic has also heightened an appreciation for physical, in-person interaction.
