# The New Inventors-20070905200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by James A. Loughlin. The show features a panel of judges, including interior designer Alison Page and engineering professor Venus Sajwala, who evaluate three new inventions. The first invention presented is a wheelie bin compactor designed by Terry Fernandez, which uses a manual lever to compress waste in standard council bins, allowing users to fit more rubbish into the same container. The panel discusses its $60 retail price, its cast iron construction weighing 11 kilograms, and its potential for weight reduction using hard plastics. The second invention is the Swift Tech 2020, a dental instrument sterilizer created by John Neville from Coffs Harbour. Inspired by pressure cooker technology, the device uses copper balls to generate steam rapidly, sterilizing instruments four to five times faster than existing machines. It features folding trays that double as sterile work surfaces and includes an auto-diagnostic tool that allows remote troubleshooting via the internet. The panel praises its speed, cost-effectiveness regarding distilled water and labor, and its ability to reduce patient waiting times. The final invention is the NumaLift plaster lifter, a pneumatic device that safely raises plaster sheets to walls and ceilings. It is designed to prevent shoulder injuries among plasterers and operates on compressed air with a failsafe mechanism that gently lowers the load if a component fails. The device costs approximately $3,000 and requires no maintenance beyond visual inspection. After deliberation, the panel unanimously selects the Swift Tech 2020 as the winner of the night, citing its originality, design intricacy, and marketability. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award and a brief preview of upcoming programs, including a news segment on the US President's visit to Sydney.
