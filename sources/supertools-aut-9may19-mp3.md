# Supertools AUT 9May19.mp3

## Summary

Talk to the AUT (Auckland, New Zealand) — 'Supertools.' Mark opens by saying he loves New Zealand and has 'an evil master plan' to both empower the audience and 'give you the sense that this is a world that you can create.' (The transcript then shifts into a related regional talk, 'The New Broom,' aimed at the Kimberley/Broome region of Western Australia.) He clarifies what a professional futurist is — 'a lot more similar to what a climate scientist does' (reads the data and trends and tells you how to lean into the coming world), not a psychic. He pitches the Kimberley as 'a canvas' already 'incredibly beautiful,' and the opportunity to translate its many stories into AUGMENTED-REALLY experiences that visitors will consume through AR spectacles coming from Apple (~2 years out), Google (a year or two), and Facebook (three to four years) — 'you won't have to sell the spectacles, just point them at the experience.' He notes it is no accident his session is bracketed by talks on cryptocurrencies and logistics — 'the most obvious points where the future is rising to meet the present' — and closes: 'This future is yours... just enough to get the doors open on an incubator and accelerator that can spin out a range of businesses that come first to the Kimberley. You have decided that you want to be the place where the future comes from first.'
