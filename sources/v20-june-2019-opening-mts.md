# V20 June 2019 Opening.MTS

## Summary

This recording is the opening address of the V20 Summit, a two-day conference focused on cryptocurrency regulation and compliance. Mark Pesce, identified as the summit chair and a pioneer in web 3D technology, delivers the welcome remarks. He begins by reflecting on his participation in the first World Wide Web conference in Geneva 25 years prior, where he helped standardize VRML for web browsers alongside collaborators Tony Parisi and Gavin Bell. Pesce notes that Bell later became the chief scientist of the Bitcoin Foundation, working on post-quantum cryptography. Using a metaphor of explorers, scouts, pioneers, and settlers, Pesce argues that the cryptocurrency industry is transitioning from an anarchic pioneering phase to a regulated, stable phase. He states that the summit’s primary goal is to provide a clear roadmap for virtual asset service providers, or VASPs, to comply with new recommendations from the Financial Action Task Force, or FATF, regarding anti-money laundering and know-your-customer regulations. Pesce outlines the two-day agenda, which includes overviews from industry and regulatory perspectives, closed sessions for VASPs, and roundtable discussions on industry challenges. He highlights Japan’s experience in regulating VASPs as a key learning opportunity. The core of the event involves three workshop sessions designed to reach a consensus on technical and procedural solutions for FATF compliance. He clarifies that consensus does not mean unanimity but rather a proposal that no one is willing to block. The program concludes with discussions on connecting with legislators and developing a multilateral approach for the future. Pesce notes that the recent announcement of Facebook’s Libra project has intensified the need for compliance, as central bankers have stressed that Libra must adhere to existing financial regulations.
