# The Unseen Internet - The Alembic 9Jan26.mp4

## Summary

This recording captures a book launch event and panel discussion held on a Friday night at the Olympic, a center for consciousness culture. The event celebrates the release of Shira Chess’s new book, The Unseen Internet: Conjuring the Occult in Digital Discourse. The host, Eric Davis, introduces the program, noting that he is a subject of the book, and mentions upcoming events at the venue, including a psychedelic salon and art exposition. Shira Chess then delivers a talk outlining her thesis that internet culture was built by individuals who believed in various forms of magic, arguing that these esoteric beliefs were baked into digital infrastructure. She traces historical parallels between 19th-century spiritualism and telegraphy, discusses the influence of 1960s and 70s counterculture figures like Timothy Leary and Terence McKenna on early computing, and details the rise of technopaganism and chaos magic in the 1990s. Chess highlights how early internet platforms like BBSs and Usenet facilitated the spread of occult texts and how the concept of "hacking reality" emerged from the intersection of hacker culture and magical thinking. Following the talk, a panel discussion takes place featuring Shira Chess, Eric Davis, Paco Nathan, Mark Pesce, and Ken Wait. The conversation centers on the concept of "consensus reality" and whether it was intentionally fractured by 1990s counterculture figures. The panelists debate the implications of this fragmentation, with some acknowledging the playful and experimental nature of their past actions while others express concern about the current state of truth and media. Mark Pesce discusses the role of AI and cybernetics, arguing that Silicon Valley’s marketing of artificial intelligence as a concentration of power is misleading. The discussion concludes with audience questions regarding the impact of AI on normative power and the nature of reality in the digital age. The book is noted to be available for pre-order, with a release date of February 10th.
