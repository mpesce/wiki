# The New Inventors-20070328195900.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by Dave O'Neill. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, including engineering professor Veena Sahajwala, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and woodworker Richard Vaughan. The first invention presented is the Hydrosolar, a passive solar panel tracker developed by Mike Patterson from Crows Nest, Queensland. Patterson explains that the device uses hydraulic fluid and radiant heat to rotate panels toward the sun without electricity, claiming efficiency increases of 40 percent in Queensland and 150 percent in Victoria compared to fixed panels. He notes the system is robust enough to withstand 40-knot winds and requires minimal recalibration. The second invention is the FAS foot alignment system, created by Neil Smith from Sydney. Smith presents a device that allows practitioners to take orthotic casts of a patient's feet while they are standing and bearing weight, rather than lying down. This method provides a more accurate representation of the foot's shape under load, allowing for better alignment of the spine and limbs. The system can also simulate the foot position inside various types of footwear, including high heels, to ensure the resulting orthotic insoles fit correctly in the user's actual shoes. The final invention is the Blowfly sander, designed by Terry Goschuck. This tool attaches to a standard drill and uses flexible abrasive strips to sand contours and curves that rigid sanders cannot reach. Goschuck demonstrates its versatility on a walking stick and notes that the abrasive strips are inexpensive and replaceable, reducing waste. The judges deliberate on the merits of each invention, with Veena Sahajwala favoring the Hydrosolar for its environmental benefits, while Bernie Hobbs and Richard Vaughan vote for the FAS foot alignment system due to its practical need and accuracy. The FAS system wins the Inventor of the Year award by a two-to-one vote. The episode concludes with a prompt for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award and a quote from Chinese writer Lin Yutang.
