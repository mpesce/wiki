# The New Inventors-20070321195900.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features a panel of three judges: agricultural scientist Chris Russell, interior designer Alison Page, and inventor Nick Morris. The episode presents three distinct inventions for evaluation. The first is the Scriber, a versatile skateboard created by Ben Lukaczyk from the Gold Coast. Lukaczyk explains that the board allows the user to adjust steering angles without tools, enabling the rider to perform wide turns, tight slaloms, and high-speed bombing on a single board. He demonstrates the mechanism, which uses extension springs and a movable pin to change the steering radius, and notes that the design prevents speed wobbles at high velocities. The second invention is a metered roller developed by Tony Burrell for treating sheep with pesticides. The device uses a roller that drips chemical in direct contact with the animal's back, measuring the dose based on the length of the sheep to ensure accurate application and reduce waste. Burrell discusses the safety benefits of limiting operator exposure to toxic chemicals and the potential for automating the process with a spring-loaded arm. The third invention is an easy hang door lifter designed by Warren Starr. This tool allows a single person to lift and position heavy doors, up to 200 kilograms, into a door jamb using foot-operated levers, a task that previously required two people. The judges deliberate on the merits of each invention regarding design, need, and market potential. Chris Russell favors the metered roller for its functional simplicity and industry importance, while Alison Page and Nick Morris support the Scriber for its innovative design and potential to create a new subculture within extreme sports. The panel ultimately selects the Scriber as the winner of the episode. The broadcast concludes with a prompt for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award and a preview of upcoming ABC programming, including a news segment regarding the Prime Minister's speech on Australian troops in Iraq.
