# The New Inventors-20091007200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes engineer James Bradfield Moody, engineering professor Venus Sahajwala, and futurist Mark Pesce. The first invention is the ImmacuCut, a vertical enclosed plasma cutter developed by Paul McCleary from Sydney. McCleary explains that his design contains sparks, fumes, and dust within a vertical table system, improving safety and space efficiency for industrial metal cutting. He notes that the vertical orientation prevents molten metal from pooling and allows for continuous operation using two tables. The second invention is the Belkers Feedaway, a foldable cardboard baby feeding chair. The presenter demonstrates how the chair assembles in under a minute and features a food-grade coating for easy cleaning. The design is intended for temporary use, such as during holidays, and is fully recyclable after its intended lifespan of two to three weeks. The third invention is a release and retrieve boat latch created by Jeff. This device allows a boat to be launched and retrieved from a trailer by a single person without entering the water. It uses a spring-loaded catch that releases when the boat moves forward and engages when the boat is driven back onto the trailer. After discussion, the judges unanimously select the ImmacuCut as the winner of the episode, citing its significant safety improvements and design efficiency. The segment concludes with a prompt for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award. The transcript also includes standard ABC1 television advertisements for programs such as Gardening Australia, Heart and Soul, and The Boondocks, as well as a news bulletin covering the resignation of former Treasurer Peter Costello, investigations into tobacco companies using social media, and a study linking supermarket competition to obesity.
