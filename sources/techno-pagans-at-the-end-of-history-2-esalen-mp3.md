# Techno-Pagans at the End of History 2(Esalen).mp3

## Summary

Part of 'Techno-Pagans at the End of History' (Esalen Institute, 2001) — a multi-day participatory weekend co-hosted by Mark Pesce, Douglas Rushkoff, and Erik Davis. The group moves between long-form conversation and open discussion with the audience, spanning virtual reality, psychedelics, nanotechnology, religion, and the end of history. (Episode 2.) Mark introduces Terence McKenna's 'time wave' theory (via a summary by a friend) and charts history into three major sections: a 67-year section (the bulk of his life), an interesting 13-month section in 2011, and a six-day section immediately preceding the 2012 winter solstice — during which 'there will be more novelty than has existed on the planet since ever.' He describes how he worked his way up from Alvin Toffler to Teilhard de Chardin to McKenna, and how the premise that 'the universe is seeking a type of closure, that things are converging' became 'a foundational fact' all his subsequent work is based on. The session closes by framing the weekend's goal as 'true humaneness in sympathetic symbiosis with the planet and with these strange children that we have brought into the world — machines,' and announces the 7:30 AM yoga in the big house across the bridge.
