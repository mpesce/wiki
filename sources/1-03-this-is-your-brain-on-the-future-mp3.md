# 1-03 This Is Your Brain on the Future.mp3

## Summary

Informal panel/discussion ('This is your brain on the future') on the vectors in the development of psychoactive DRUGS and psychoactive TECHNOLOGIES — what it means for humans to gain increasing control over their mindset, brain, and emotions. Mark sets the baseline with the 'growing pharmaceuticalization of mind states': the 20th century's expansion of available psychoactives, and the last 20-40 years' huge expansion of things normal people can use to change how they feel — acutely ('I'd like to feel better right now') and over the long term (antidepressants you take daily for a month, hoping four to six weeks later you're happier and less irritable). The conversation turns to the recursive problem of the SELF and 'baseline': one could argue a true baseline is only the tiny period from birth until imprinting starts, because 'once you started imprinting on whatever your culture is, you're off baseline.' The discussion frames a 'ground state' — a mode that conserves the legacy of everything that comprises you — and the opportunity to play different modalities of being (height of passion, great duress, boredom, important work) 'off one another, iron [them] against one another, and try to find this ground state for yourself.' It ends on the singularity and the open question of who we are individually and collectively — 'the question of who we are is really very much open.'
