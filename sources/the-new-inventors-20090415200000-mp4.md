# The New Inventors-20090415200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by a panel that includes judges James and Alison. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a live studio audience. The first invention is an interchangeable surfboard designed by Jared Smith from Wollongong. Smith explains that the board consists of a fixed front section and swappable rear tails, allowing surfers to adapt to different wave conditions without carrying multiple boards. The joint uses carbon fiber and can be secured with a standard coin, making it convenient for travel and airline baggage restrictions. The second invention is a road maintenance attachment created by Dave and Wally Challen from Mansfield, Victoria. Their device is a blade that attaches to a conventional front-end loader, enabling a single machine to clear roadside drains and scoop up debris. This replaces the need for two separate expensive machines, a grader and a loader, thereby reducing costs and time for local councils. The inventors note the device was used extensively during recent bushfire cleanup efforts in northeastern Victoria. The third invention is a compression jar created by Peter Lucaris, designed to keep coffee, tea, and spices fresh. Unlike vacuum-seal jars that suck air out, this jar uses a mechanical lid that pushes air out of the container when tightened, creating a compressed airtight seal. The panel discusses the market potential and design of each invention. The judges ultimately select the drain blade as the winner for the night, citing its practical impact on road safety and maintenance efficiency. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award and a quote from Steve Jobs regarding innovation.
