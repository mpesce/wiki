# The New Inventors-20070808200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features a panel of judges, including agricultural scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and woodworking teacher Richard Vaughan, who evaluate three distinct inventions. The episode begins with a segment on the Aussie Mochi Jig, a doweling tool presented by Des Fenelon from Sydney. The device allows users to assemble a jig, clamp it to timber, and drill holes without pencil marks, ensuring precise alignment for wood joints. The panel discusses its versatility compared to biscuit joiners, with Fenelon noting a price point of approximately 200 Australian dollars. The second invention is the Hahn Easy Wheel Lift, demonstrated by John Hahn from Foster, New South Wales. This inflatable bag uses air pressure from the spare tire itself to lift the wheel onto a vehicle, eliminating the need for heavy manual lifting. Hahn explains that the device uses a small amount of air from the tire to inflate the bag, allowing for easy maneuvering and bolting. The judges praise the passive energy source and its potential to prevent injury, particularly for four-wheel drive drivers and older users. The final invention is the Smart Console, a touch-sensitive control panel developed by Michael Stavrou. Stavrou, who has worked on the project since 1979, presents a curved interface that allows users to control audio mixing, lighting, and other functions by touching specific zones, reducing the need for physical movement and complex menus. The panel highlights its applications in music recording and industrial control, noting its modular design and ergonomic benefits. The judges ultimately select the Smart Console as the winner of the episode, citing its elegance and inventiveness, while viewers are invited to vote for the People's Choice Award. The transcript concludes with a quote from Neil Young and a transition to the next segment.
