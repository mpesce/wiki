## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features a panel of judges, including agricultural scientist Chris Russell, interior designer Alison Page, and woodworker Richard Vaughan, who evaluate various new inventions. The episode begins with a promotional segment for the psychological thriller The Best Man, starring Keeley Hawes and Richard Coyle, airing on September 25. The first invention presented is the Sun Lizard, a solar-powered climate control system developed by Colin Gillum. The device is installed on a roof to harvest solar energy for both heating and cooling. In winter mode, the system draws warm air from near the ceiling, heats it to 50 degrees Celsius, and circulates it near the floor, raising room temperature by approximately 30 degrees. In summer mode, it extracts hot air from the room and vents it outside, potentially cooling the interior by up to 10 degrees. The system is self-regulating and shuts off automatically at night. Gillum explains that the unit costs about 2,500 dollars plus 1,000 dollars for installation and is designed to heat an area of 10 to 12 square meters, though efficiency depends on the home's insulation. The judges note that the system is particularly effective in temperate climates and appreciate that it avoids the drying effects of traditional blow heaters. The second invention is the Barrow Tipper, a device designed to help a single person safely empty a wheelbarrow into a skip bin.
