## Summary

Clip on 'relative superintelligence.' If you run several systems and one is brighter than the others (and brighter than you and most of the people you could bring to judge it), then for that domain it is relatively superintelligent. Example: 'Mythos' is really good at finding bugs, chaining them into exploits, and its abilities are better than a small circle of cyber experts.
