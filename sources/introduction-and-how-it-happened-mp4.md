## Summary

This recording is a talk delivered by Mark Pesce, an author, researcher, engineer, and futurist, at an event titled 2035. The host introduces Pesce, noting his role as one of the inventors of the metaverse, before welcoming him to the stage. Pesce begins his address by setting the temporal context, stating that the audience is currently in January 2035. He announces that humanity has successfully solved the major problem of climate change, a development that might seem surprising when looking back at the early 2020s. Pesce explains that the rapid decarbonization of Australia and the broader global transition were not isolated events but the result of roots extending back approximately thirty years to the early years of the millennium. He argues that understanding this transformation requires examining how it occurred across three distinct scales: the domestic sphere, the industrial sphere, and the global level. The speaker suggests that the solutions implemented in the 2030s were built upon foundations laid much earlier, challenging the perception that the change happened solely in the last decade or fifteen years. The talk focuses on the historical trajectory of this technological and societal shift, emphasizing the long-term nature of the progress made in addressing environmental challenges.
