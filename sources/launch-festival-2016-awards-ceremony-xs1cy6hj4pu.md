# LAUNCH Festival 2016 Awards Ceremony-XS1CY6hJ4pU.mp4

## Summary

This recording captures the awards ceremony segment of the Launch Festival 2016, hosted by Jason Calacanis. The event features a grand jury that deliberated over presentations from the demo pit and other categories to select winners for thirteen distinct awards. Calacanis introduces the process, noting the seriousness of the competition and the involvement of a photographer for winner portraits. He also acknowledges the technical team from McCune Sound for providing flawless audio and video production in a challenging venue. The ceremony proceeds with the announcement of winners across various categories, including Best Hardware for Line Health, Best Design for Flutter, and Best Social Good for Flow Labs. Other recipients include Stay Wonderful for Best Business Model, Evertoon for Best Presentation, and Daily Drip for Best Education. New categories introduced this year include Fintech, won by Quantiax, and Best Demo Pit, awarded to Carbon Robotics for their affordable robotic arm. Additional awards go to Wistick for Enterprise, Guardian Circle for Diamond in the Rough, Bloom for Best Health Startup, and Betagate for the Hackathon category. Betagate’s win also secures them an invitation to the Launch Incubator, with other hackathon participants also receiving invitations pending due diligence. A significant portion of the event is dedicated to a special announcement involving Mark Pesce, a longtime friend of Calacanis and co-host of This Week in Startups Australia. Calacanis reveals that the Launch Festival will expand to Australia within the next year, with the specific city to be announced in two weeks following a competition between Sydney and Melbourne. The event concludes with the Best Overall award going to Cafe X and the IBM Smart Camp winner, Car Force, who also receives an incubator spot. The final award, Best Alumni Update, is presented to Jeff Dachshus of One Drop. Calacanis closes by thanking his team and encouraging the audience to pursue their entrepreneurial ideas.
