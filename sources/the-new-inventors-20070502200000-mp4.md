# The New Inventors-20070502200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, featuring a panel of judges evaluating three distinct inventions. The judges for the evening are agricultural scientist Chris Russell, interior designer Alison Page, and woodworker Richard Vaughan. The first invention presented is the WDV 3000, a ventilating skylight designed by Shane West. This device uses an aerodynamic foil to swing open in the wind, utilizing negative pressure to extract hot air while simultaneously allowing natural light into attics or rooms. The judges noted its dual function of ventilation and lighting, its low cost of approximately 150 dollars, and its 15-year warranty on the polycarbonate material, though they questioned its effectiveness in heavy rain. The second invention is the Burrow Bib, created by John Simphendorf. Inspired by the movement of a burrowing beetle, this attachment clamps onto the front of a standard wheelbarrow. It allows the user to scoop soil, sand, or mulch into the barrow by pushing it forward, eliminating the need for manual shoveling. The judges praised the originality of the concept and its potential for the landscaping market, but raised concerns about the robustness of the clamps and whether the effort required to push the loaded barrow would be significantly less than using a shovel. The final invention is the Easy On and Off Silo Lid, presented by Jo Burd. This device uses a winch mechanism at the base of a grain silo to lift the lid horizontally and lock it in place, preventing farmers from having to climb ladders or lift heavy lids manually. This design addresses safety concerns and prevents birds from accessing the grain. The judges unanimously selected the silo lid as the winner of the episode, citing the critical need for safety improvements in the agricultural industry and the simplicity of the design modification. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote in the People's Choice Award and a brief preview of upcoming segments.
