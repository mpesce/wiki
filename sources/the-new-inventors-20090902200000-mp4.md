## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by Fiona. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, including agricultural scientist Chris Russell and interior designer Alison Page. The first segment introduces Micah Atkin from Melbourne, who presents a handheld point-of-care blood analysis device. This portable laboratory uses single-use cards to provide instant results for conditions such as tuberculosis, HIV, and influenza, eliminating the need for traditional laboratory processing and refrigeration. The second inventor demonstrates a vehicle safety system called Synchro Multi Eyes, which utilizes cameras to eliminate blind spots by displaying side and rear views on a monitor when the driver toggles the indicator handle. The third invention is a battery-operated tool designed to rapidly apply protective spiral wrap to hydraulic hoses, saving time and preventing damage in rough industrial environments. Following the demonstrations, the judges deliberate on the merits of each invention. They discuss the critical need for rapid medical diagnostics in developing regions, the safety benefits of improved vehicle visibility, and the practical simplicity of the hose wrapping tool. The panel unanimously selects the handheld blood analysis device as the winner of the night, citing its potential to save lives and its innovative design. The episode concludes with an announcement that the winning inventor is now in the running for the Inventor of the Year award, followed by a brief news segment covering Australian economic growth, a submarine contamination scare, and a major earthquake in Indonesia.
