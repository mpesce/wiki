# The New Inventors-20070523200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by journalists Bernie Hobbs and engineer James Bradfield Moody. The show features three inventors presenting their innovations to a panel of judges. The first invention is Water Fresh, presented by Tony Dixon, a system that reuses sewerage by using a cell destruction unit to pulverize bacteria with extreme pressure fluctuations before adding chlorine dioxide for disinfection. Dixon explains that this physical and chemical process is more compact than biological systems, requires less chemical usage, and can service up to 6,000 people in developing regions. The second invention is the Quick Lock curtain system, presented by Lindsay King, a pneumatic device for trucks that replaces manual belts and buckles with hooks and cam systems to secure curtains in seconds rather than minutes. King notes the system saves drivers significant time daily and is designed for new rigid body trucks rather than retrofits. The third invention is the Southern Reflector, presented by Derek Wrigley, a device made of polished stainless steel panels that reflects sunlight and heat into south-facing rooms to improve lighting and warmth. Wrigley states the invention is not patented and can raise indoor temperatures by up to eight degrees in winter. The judges, including Chris and Debbie, debate the merits of the three inventions based on criteria such as originality, need, design, and marketability. While some judges favor the Quick Lock system for its simplicity and clear market demand, others argue for Water Fresh due to its innovative combination of technologies and scalability. Ultimately, the panel votes for Water Fresh as the winner of the episode, with Tony Dixon receiving a trophy and entering the running for the annual Inventor of the Year award. The episode concludes with an announcement for a People's Choice Award vote and a teaser for the following week's special episode focused on water-saving innovations. The transcript ends with a brief promotional segment for a different show, Specs and Specs, featuring a classical violinist and a rock musician.
