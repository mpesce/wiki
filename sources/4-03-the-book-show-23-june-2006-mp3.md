# 4-03 The Book Show 23 June 2006.mp3

## Summary

ABC Radio National 'The Book Show' (Romana Koval, 23 June 2006). A panel of experts (including Mark) discusses storytelling and writing in computer games: 'more and more games are exploring sophisticated narrative elements as film-savvy players expect more in terms of character development and storyline.' Players now choose how their characters dress and interact; 'choice is the new buzzword'; cutscenes (one-to-two-minute 3D animated clips) advance the plot and present character development. 'Players now want to know the motives behind the characters' actions. It's no longer a case of shooting because he's a bad guy.' The segment ends with the show's standard book reviews (including Richard E. Grant's diary about making a film) and previews of upcoming guests.
