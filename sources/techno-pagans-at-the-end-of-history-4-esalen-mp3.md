# Techno-Pagans at the End of History 4(Esalen).mp3

## Summary

Part of 'Techno-Pagans at the End of History' (Esalen Institute, 2001) — a multi-day participatory weekend co-hosted by Mark Pesce, Douglas Rushkoff, and Erik Davis. The group moves between long-form conversation and open discussion with the audience, spanning virtual reality, psychedelics, nanotechnology, religion, and the end of history. (Episode 4.) Mark describes watching TVision — a static geological view of the planet plus live meteorological data — and how 'something inside me simply snapped. This was the vision I'd had in 1990.' He predicts the globe on every teacher's desk will be replaced by a live model of the planet that 'will become conflated with our understanding of the planet' — the first thing that goes away is political division ('there's no room for it in that model. What you see is the organic unity of the Gaian logos'). The conversation then turns to psychedelics and Eros: the idea that a large family of alcohol and psychoactive agents stimulate CNS arousal, and that in primates that means 'erection and hanky panky.' Mark reflects on his own experiences — a mild, pleasant ayahuasca (no ego death), and a lifelong difficulty with mushrooms (psilocybin) because they push on his emotional center, whereas acid is 'a very intellectual sort of mind experience.'
