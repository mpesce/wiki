## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges consisting of agricultural scientist Chris Russell, engineering professor Veena Sahajwala, and designer Alison Page. The first invention is the Super Scooper, a device designed to collect horse manure from paddocks. Mark Bridgman demonstrates how the unit, which attaches to a ride-on mower or quad bike, scoops waste and then converts into a trailer for transport. The judges praise its simplicity and low cost, noting it solves a common and unpleasant chore for horse owners. The second invention is the Verso measuring lid, presented by an inventor who describes it as a combination lid and measuring device for granules, powders, and liquids. It uses an airlock mechanism to dispense exact doses without removing the lid, offering a user-friendly alternative to traditional measuring spoons. The panel discusses its potential applications in medicine, cooking, and industrial settings, noting that different inserts could be used for various volumes and weights. The third invention is the Fire Exit Paver, a modular LED paving light by Mike Kalinowski that doubles as an evacuation aid. Wired into a building's fire alarm system, the pavers display animated green arrows to guide people to safety during an emergency, while serving as decorative lighting under normal conditions. The judges highlight the design's versatility and its ability to improve upon standard emergency lighting requirements. The episode concludes with the judges selecting the Super Scooper as the winner of the night, citing its robustness and immediate market appeal. Viewers are invited to vote for the People's Choice Award via text or phone.
