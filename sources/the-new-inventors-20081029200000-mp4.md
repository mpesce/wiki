# The New Inventors-20081029200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation program The New Inventors, specifically the 200th installment, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, including agricultural scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Benny Hobbs, and inventor and journalist Christine Kinenmo. The episode begins with a brief retrospective on the show's five-year history, noting that 597 Australian inventions and 717 inventors have been featured. The first inventor, Dean Cameron, presents Join Locks, a flexible joining system inspired by his previous invention, the biolitics. The system uses interlocking edges and a locking key to join materials quickly without nuts and bolts, allowing for compact transport and easy assembly of items like water tanks and pressure vessels. The second inventor, Aldo Contarino from Perth, introduces the Rear Sorb, a shock absorber bushing system that uses a hyper-elastic elastomer to absorb impact and provide overload protection in a single moving part. The third inventor is 14-year-old Rachel Latimer from Wagga Wagga, who presents the Easy Girth, a pulley-based system designed to help riders, particularly those with limited strength, tighten horse saddle straps independently. The panel evaluates the inventions based on design, safety, and originality. While the judges acknowledge the strengths of each invention, they ultimately vote for Join Locks as the winner of the night, citing its universal applications, simplicity, and cost-effectiveness. The episode concludes with an announcement that the public can vote for the People's Choice Award via text, phone, or the show's website. The transcript ends with a brief transition to a different program, Speak Suspect, hosted by Adam Hills.
