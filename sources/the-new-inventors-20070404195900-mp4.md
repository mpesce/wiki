# The New Inventors-20070404195900.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation program The New Inventors, hosted by James Ray Lachlan. The show features a panel of experts, including engineering professor Veena Sahajwala, agricultural scientist Chris Russell, and designer Sally Deminks, who evaluate three new inventions. The first invention presented is the Clever Peg, a double-ended clothes peg developed by Ian Rankin and Miranda Strickland from Perth. The inventors explain that their design, which uses a Z-shaped spring made of acetyl nylon with shape memory, allows for double the hanging capacity and improved durability compared to standard pegs. The panel discusses the material choices and wind resistance, with the inventors demonstrating that the spring remains embedded in the plastic if the peg is broken, reducing choking hazards. The second invention is the Speedy Eave, a ceiling panel installation system created by Kevin Williams and Alan Tyler from Mossville. This tool is designed to simplify the installation of fibro panels by allowing them to slide into a groove without the fiddling required by traditional H-molds. The inventors demonstrate how the system saves four to five hours per house and addresses issues with noggins and corners. The panel notes that while the design is a significant improvement, it currently has limitations regarding specific building structures and paint adhesion on plastic cover strips. The final invention is the Triac, a three-piece sit-on-top kayak invented by Owen Griffin from Brisbane. The kayak is designed to be easily assembled and dismantled using a ratchet strap system that threads through sealed tubes, eliminating the need for bolts or heavy lifting. Each piece is buoyant, allowing for assembly in the water. The panel praises the simplicity of the belt mechanism and the modular design, which allows the kayak to convert from a single to a double-person vessel. The episode concludes with the judges selecting the Clever Peg as the winner, followed by a segment for the audience to vote for the People's Choice Award.
