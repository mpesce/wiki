# The New Inventors-20071107200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by Adam Hills. The show features three inventors presenting their products to a panel of judges, including agricultural scientist Chris Russell, engineering professor Venus Sahajwala, and interior designer Alison Page. The first invention presented is the AquaFlex cooler system by Dave Davies from Perth, which replaces traditional heavy plastic water bottles with sealed, metalized bladders containing 11.5 liters of water. Davies argues that the system is more hygienic, easier to transport, and offers consumers a 20 percent cost saving. The judges discuss the durability of the bags and the logistics of recycling the metalized polymer material, noting that while the concept is sound, the market benefit must be clear to replace the existing bottle infrastructure. The second invention is a precision sharpening guide by Peter Collins from Newcastle, designed for woodworking tools. The device holds a chisel or gouge in place while a sharpening stone moves over the edge, using a protractor to set exact angles and eliminate human error. Collins, a boat builder, demonstrates the ease of use, though the judges raise concerns about safety regarding finger placement and the high price point of 450 dollars, which may limit its appeal to the mass market compared to cheaper electric sharpeners. The final invention is the Cultihoe, a multi-functional gardening tool by Eric and Rayleigh Rose from Albany, Western Australia. This single tool incorporates five different implements, including a digging hoe, weeder, scarifier, furrow maker, and paver cleaner, allowing users to switch functions by applying pressure to change the blade position. The judges praise the tool’s clever design, light weight, and affordable price point of under 50 dollars. After deliberation, the panel votes for the Cultihoe as the winner of the night, citing its originality and practical market potential. The episode concludes with a recap of the previous week’s People’s Choice award, which went to the weightless wheelbarrow, and a preview of the upcoming segment Specs, featuring guests such as Elinora Roccobaro and Dave O’Neill.
