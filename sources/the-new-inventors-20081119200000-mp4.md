# The New Inventors-20081119200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features a panel of judges, including agricultural scientist Chris Russell, designer Sally Deminks, and futurist Mark Pesci, who evaluate four distinct inventions. The episode begins with a presentation by John Shamansky and Wayne Baddick, who demonstrate a buoyant, waterproof barrel designed to store marine rescue equipment. The device allows boaters to tether the barrel to themselves in the event of a capsizing, providing flotation and access to emergency beacons, flares, and radios. The judges praise the design for its compactness and safety, noting that it forces essential gear to be kept on the deck for instant access. The second invention is the Fusseggers walking stick, created by 12-year-old Alana for her grandfather who suffers from arthritis. The multi-purpose stick incorporates a hammer and storage compartments for maps and sample bags, intended for gold prospecting. Following this, Stephen Wilson presents Stepwise, a smart card technology aimed at reducing online credit card fraud. By using a chip and PIN verification similar to EFTPOS terminals, the system prevents the transmission of sensitive personal data to merchants, thereby protecting users from identity theft. The judges discuss the potential for widespread adoption and the security benefits of keeping personal information with the bank rather than the merchant. The final invention is Quiet Wave, a noise-dampening membrane developed by Philippe Donneux. The system uses an organic, elasticized material derived from food industry residue, which is sandwiched between wall panels to absorb sound energy. Donneux demonstrates that the material significantly reduces noise transmission and is lighter than traditional soundproofing solutions. The judges unanimously select Quiet Wave as the winner of the episode, citing its originality, environmental benefits, and effectiveness. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote in the People's Choice Award and a preview of the upcoming grand final.
