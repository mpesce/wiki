# The New Inventors-20071031200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by Adam Hills. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes agricultural scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Bernie Hodges, and interior designer Alison Page. The episode begins with a brief preview of other ABC programs, including a documentary on Captain Cook and a series on librarians, before transitioning to the main segment. The first inventor, Karl Price from Tasmania, presents a diver recovery vest that combines a recovery harness, buoyancy aid, and weight system into a single unit. He demonstrates how the vest can be quickly removed and donned, highlighting its safety benefits for commercial and Navy divers. The second inventor, Darryl Schneider, introduces the ISO lock, a welding electrode holder that mechanically isolates the power supply when the electrode is removed, preventing electrocution. The third inventor, Richard Morris from New South Wales, showcases the weightless wheelbarrow, which uses a gear system and in-line wheels to reduce the physical effort required to push and unload the device. The judges debate the merits of the three inventions, considering factors such as originality, design, marketability, and safety. Alison Page favors the ISO lock for its originality, while Chris Russell and Bernie Hodges lean toward the diver recovery vest due to its clear market need and safety improvements. The judges ultimately select Karl Price’s diver recovery vest as the winner of the episode. The program concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the People’s Choice Award and a final sign-off by Adam Hills.
