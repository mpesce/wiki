# The New Inventors-20071024200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features a panel of judges, including agricultural scientist Chris Russell, designer Sally Deminks, and woodworker Richard Vaughan, who evaluate three inventions designed to address dangerous activities. The first invention presented is the Petrol Also, a safer alternative to chainsaws and disc cutters developed by Kevin Inkster from Perth. The device uses two blades in an alternating stepping motion to cut wood and masonry without exposed cutting edges on the back, making it tolerant of dirt and easier to sharpen. The judges praised its revolutionary design and safety features, noting it addresses the high injury rate associated with traditional power tools. The second invention is the Nail Desk Vent, created by Alan, which is built into nail salon desks to remove noxious fumes and dust from acrylic nail work. The system uses a fan and filters to capture filings and absorb vapors, significantly improving air quality for technicians and clients. The panel acknowledged the genuine need for this solution, with one judge noting their personal sensitivity to polish fumes. The third invention is the Ladder Smart, a gutter-mounted ladder stabilizer designed by Barry to prevent wobbling and falls during routine maintenance. It clamps onto the gutter profile to secure the ladder, protecting both the user and the gutter from damage. The judges highlighted its simplicity and the significant safety benefit for homeowners and tradespeople who frequently work at heights. The episode concludes with the judges selecting the Petrol Also as the winner for the night, citing its originality and potential to replace dangerous tools. The host then announces that the winner will be in the running for the Bright Spark Young Inventor of the Year award, though the segment also featured a young inventor named Alex presenting a paper airplane launcher. The broadcast ends with a promotion for the People's Choice Award and a teaser for the following week's episode of Speaks and Specs.
