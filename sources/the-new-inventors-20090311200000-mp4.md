# The New Inventors-20090311200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is a broadcast of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The episode features four inventors presenting their products to a panel of judges, including Sally Deminks, Christine Kininmuth, and Richard Vaughan. The first invention is Aqualim, a water-saving shower controller developed by Trevor Murphy and Ken Harrison. The device uses a hydraulic motor to measure and limit water volume to a preset amount, such as 26 liters, without requiring electricity. The judges praised its mechanical design and potential for international markets, particularly in countries with less advanced water conservation infrastructure. The second invention is the Rower Go B-race, a device by Michael and Steve that attaches to rowing machines to prevent full leg extension. This forces users to maintain proper posture, reducing the risk of back pain which affects a significant percentage of rowers. The panel noted its high quality construction and immediate market need, with the inventors reporting they had already sold 65 units. The third invention is the Bin Carrier, created by seven-year-old Aidan, a metal rack for the back of a car that allows drivers to transport bins without walking long distances. The final invention is the Carportable, a portable carport that assembles in 12 minutes and fits in a car boot. It uses water-filled bags for stability and features a trampoline-like cover to deflect hail. The panel discussed its versatility and cost, noting it is cheaper than permanent structures. The judges ultimately awarded the win to Aqualim, citing its originality and significant market need. The broadcast also includes promotional segments for other ABC One programs, such as The Making of Modern Australia and Before the News, as well as news items regarding rugby league suspensions and a criminal trial. The episode concludes with a quote from Isaac Newton and information on how viewers can vote for the People's Choice Award.
