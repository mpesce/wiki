# Stacking Up PNG in the Cloud 20Aug19.mp3

## Summary

Address on the future opportunities in Papua New Guinea (20 Aug 2019), opening with an acknowledgment of the Gadigal people of the Eora nation. He reports that the future he outlined two years ago 'is here, it's already accelerating': cargo and passenger drones are becoming a thing (drones are already inspecting palm-oil plantations in PNG); there are now more than 4 billion smartphone users globally, and PNG is heading toward a million smartphones and over a million broadband subscribers; nearly half the country will have 3G access. The 'biggest of those accelerants' is one of the biggest infrastructure projects in PNG's history (the Kumul and Coral Sea cables). He then turns to DIGITAL CURRENCIES: 'there's a whole race on to get these digital currencies out... the currency wars have begun, and the next 10 years are going to see them raging.' His argument: because PNG is going to be well connected, it's 'also going to be cashed up with digital currencies, and that will transform Papua New Guinea as much as, if not more than, any cable connection.' He urges them to 'strategize, plan, experiment, so that as the digital currencies arrive, they serve Papua New Guinea — not just the makers of these currencies, not just the Facebooks and the People's Bank of China.'
