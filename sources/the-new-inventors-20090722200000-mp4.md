# The New Inventors-20090722200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The panel of judges includes Chris Russell, engineering professor Venus Sahajwala, and futurist and author Mark Pesce. The show features three inventors presenting their creations, with the audience voting for a People's Choice Award. The first invention is the V100, a personal man-overboard safety device that transmits GPS coordinates and a mayday message via standard marine VHF radio to nearby vessels, bypassing the need to contact land-based rescue services. The inventor demonstrates the device, which activates upon contact with water, and explains that it updates location data every five minutes for the first thirty minutes. The second invention is a fold-out handrail system for patient transport buses, designed to assist elderly and disabled passengers in safely disembarking by providing external support where internal rails are insufficient. The third invention is an air cleaning system for spray booths that uses a funnel and pyramid design to separate paint particles from toxic gas, allowing gas filters to function without clogging. The judges deliberate on the criteria of need, design, and originality. Chris Russell favors the bus rail for its unique solution to a common problem, while Venus Sahajwala highlights the integrated design of the V100. Mark Pesce votes for the V100, citing its potential to set new safety benchmarks and improve current rescue practices. The episode concludes with the announcement of the winner and a quote from John F. Kennedy regarding the future. The broadcast is interspersed with promotional segments for other ABC programs, including a documentary on Salman Rushdie, a Catherine Hepburn season, and a feature on environmentally sound architecture.
