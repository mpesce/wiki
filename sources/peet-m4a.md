# Peet.m4a

## Summary

This recording is a presentation and subsequent Q&A session delivered by Mark Pesce to the board of Pete, a home builder, in Perth, Australia. Introduced by chairman Tony Lennon, Pesce, who notes he was labeled a futurist after hosting the ABC show The New Inventors, outlines three major technological trends: the increase in human capacity through connectivity, the rapid advancement of machine learning, and the democratization of simulation via virtual and augmented reality. He argues that Pete is well-positioned to leverage these trends rather than fight them. Pesce proposes that Pete integrate Amazon Alexa into its sales process, allowing the AI to recommend neighborhoods based on deep customer data and lifestyle profiles. He suggests using augmented reality, similar to the IKEA Place app or Pokemon Go, to allow potential buyers to visualize and co-design homes and communities before construction begins. Furthermore, he advocates for embedding community social media networks into new developments to foster local connections and ensure the developer remains an insider rather than an outsider. He also highlights emerging opportunities in sustainability, specifically partnering with West Australian company Power Ledger to facilitate peer-to-peer solar energy trading, and adapting to millennial preferences for urban density, flexibility, and mobility through models like WeLive and Loftium. The session concludes with a discussion on data privacy, where Pesce contrasts Amazon’s restrained data usage with Facebook’s controversial practices, urging Pete to maintain transparency. He also touches on the evolution of financial instruments, predicting that cryptocurrencies and new banking models will soon support partial home ownership and flexible mortgage structures to accommodate the mobile lifestyles of younger generations. The talk emphasizes a five-to-ten-year horizon for implementing these strategies to position Pete as a leader in community co-development.
