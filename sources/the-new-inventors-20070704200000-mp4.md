# The New Inventors-20070704200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by agricultural scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and inventor and designer Sally Deminks. The show features three inventors presenting their creations for a chance to win the Bright Sparks award. The first segment introduces Easy As, a device developed by Barry Hildebrandt Jr. and his father to assist in putting on compression stockings painlessly. Barry demonstrates the applicator on his own leg, which has a tropical ulcer, explaining that the tool allows the garment to spool onto the limb without dragging or causing pain, addressing a common compliance issue for elderly patients. The second invention is a freshwater weed harvester presented by Gary Lane and Chris O'Toole. Their pontoon-mounted device uses a cutting arm and a suction system to remove aquatic weeds from rivers, capable of harvesting down to five meters deep. Unlike existing harvesters that leave cuttings to regenerate, this machine collects over ninety percent of the vegetation into a bag, where the weight of the water compacts the weeds. The inventors note that the pump is designed to allow fish and other marine life to pass through unharmed. The final invention is the Tornado Rain Head, created by Gary Ewan. This funnel-shaped attachment for box gutters creates a vortex to move water faster into downpipes, preventing overflow and airlocks. Ewan demonstrates the device with water and leaves, showing how the vortex clears debris effectively. The panel debates the merits of the three inventions, with Russell favoring the Easy As for its originality and marketability, while Hobbs and Deminks express strong interest in the weed harvester and the rain head. The judges unanimously select the Easy As as the winner of the episode, and the show concludes with a prompt for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award via text message or phone.
