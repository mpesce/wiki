# The New Inventors-20070425200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, which includes engineer James Bradfield Moody, interior designer Allison Page, and author and futurist Mark Pesce. The first invention presented is an adjustable air duct by Ron Byhelfer from Melbourne, designed to navigate obstacles like pipes without requiring a return to the workshop for custom manufacturing. The judges discuss its potential for mass production and its utility in remote areas where on-site adjustments are difficult. The second invention is the Eco Switch, created by Rod Shepherd from Marysville, which addresses the issue of standby power consumption. Shepherd demonstrates a plug-in device that allows users to completely cut power to a power board, ensuring that appliances are truly off rather than in standby mode. The judges note the simplicity of the design and its potential for significant energy savings, particularly in countries like the United States where wall outlets lack built-in switches. They advise Shepherd to provide clear user guidance and consider adding protective fuses for delicate equipment. The final invention is the shin fin, designed by Mark Lee from Sydney, with a demonstration by 2006 Australian Iron Woman champion Naomi Flood. Unlike traditional foot flippers, this device attaches to the lower leg via rubber straps, allowing for a more natural swimming kick that reduces strain on the ankles and calf muscles. The judges praise its originality and safety benefits, noting that it allows users to walk normally and prevents the awkward backward movement required when entering the water with foot flippers. In the final deliberation, the judges vote two to one to award the win to Mark Lee and his shin fin, citing its superior user comfort and market potential. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award.
