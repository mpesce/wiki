## Summary

Clip on his own thinking: you now have an immutable, auditable record of the agent's actions, and that auditable record needs to be reviewed by an ADVERSARIAL judge — whether a human or an LLC. Either way, it has to be 'sticking it to the agent' all the time, because that's how you guarantee (to some degree of confidence) that the agent is doing what you think it's doing when you think it's doing it.
