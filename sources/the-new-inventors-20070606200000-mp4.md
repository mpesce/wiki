# The New Inventors-20070606200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges consisting of agricultural scientist Chris Russell, inventor Nick Morris, and journalist Christine Cadenmont. The first invention is the Snippin' Mow, presented by Philip Trefowin from Sydney. It is a plastic shield with four wheels that converts a standard line trimmer into a lightweight lawnmower, designed for small backyards where full-sized mowers are impractical. The device cuts grass at an even height and costs between $30 and $40. The judges noted its value for terrace houses but raised concerns about the manual process of replacing trimmer line and the potential for user fatigue. The second invention is the IV Pod, a portable telescopic IV pole invented by nurse Catherine Connor from Perth. Designed for emergency roadside medical care, the device uses gravity to administer fluids to accident victims without requiring a bystander to hold the bag or a tree to hang it from. The prototype, made of marine steel, features a spike leg for stability on soft ground and is expected to retail for $190. The judges praised its life-saving potential in remote areas and on mine sites, though they questioned the high cost compared to standard camera monopods. The final invention is the Seagrass-Friendly Mooring, presented by Des Maslin. This system replaces traditional chain and weight moorings, which scour the seabed and destroy seagrass, with a screw-in anchor point. It utilizes a shock absorber that forces seawater through a small gap to provide elasticity, protecting the marine environment. The judges unanimously selected this invention as the winner of the episode, citing its originality, environmental benefit, and ability to increase mooring density in marinas. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award and information about the Bright Spark Award for young inventors.
