# Techno-Pagans at the End of History 1(Esalen).mp3

## Summary

Part of 'Techno-Pagans at the End of History' (Esalen Institute, 2001) — a multi-day participatory weekend co-hosted by Mark Pesce, Douglas Rushkoff, and Erik Davis. The group moves between long-form conversation and open discussion with the audience, spanning virtual reality, psychedelics, nanotechnology, religion, and the end of history. (Episode 1, introductory.) The session is 'strictly introductory': the three go around the circle so people can meet, share interests and motivations, and 'what brings you to this particular watering hole this evening.' Erik Davis notes the motivation for the event and that he and Mark 'didn't remember ever meeting' — either a 1994 SIGGRAPH conversation or a mix-up with a shorter friend of Mark's. The group introduces themselves: Mark is 'the gentle giant of virtual reality, the man who hacked VRML.' Topics raised around the circle include DMT, processional astronomy, the Mayan 2012 calendar (John Major Jenkins's 'Maya CosmoGenesis'), and the difference between following a spiritual path (pedanta, yoga) and the 'free lunch' of a mushroom.
