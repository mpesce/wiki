# The New Inventors-20081022200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin on ABC One. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges consisting of engineering professor Veena Sahajwala, interior designer Alison Page, and woodworker Richard Vaughan. The episode begins with a segment on the history of the fire extinguisher and the accidental discovery of saccharine by chemist Konstantin Falberg in 1879. The first inventor, Dr. Vivian Robinson, presents an electronic water purifier designed to address the global issue of 1.1 billion people lacking access to safe drinking water. The device uses electro-coagulation to capture pollutants and pathogens, costing approximately two cents per liter to operate. Robinson demonstrates the unit, which comes in individual, family, and village sizes, and discusses the power requirements, including a wind-up option. The second inventor, Alison Deak, introduces a specialized cot sheet designed to prevent Sudden Infant Death Syndrome. The sheet is tailored to the height of the baby rather than the cot, ensuring the infant sleeps at the base of the cot to reduce the risk of suffocation from excess bedding. The third inventor, Adrienne and Ben, present a two-piece patio table that attaches to existing posts using wing nuts, allowing for easy assembly and removal for outdoor entertaining. The judges evaluate the inventions based on originality, marketability, need, and safety. Veena Sahajwala highlights the originality of the baby sheet and the innovative design of the water purifier, while Alison Page favors the versatility of the patio table. Richard Vaughan notes the simplicity of the table design. Ultimately, the panel unanimously selects the electronic water purifier as the winner due to the critical global need for clean water. The episode concludes with a mention of the previous week's People's Choice winner, the Easy Bouquet, and transitions into a news broadcast segment covering political controversies and economic inflation.
