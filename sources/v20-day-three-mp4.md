# V20 Day Three.mp4

## Summary

This recording is the final day of the V20 Summit for 2020, a virtual conference focused on virtual asset regulation and compliance. Mark Pesce opens the session by introducing the day’s agenda, which centers on technical solutions for the FATF travel rule and the InterVASP messaging protocol. Anson Seo, chair of the International Digital Asset Exchange Association, provides context on the summit’s progress, highlighting the successful collaboration between industry and regulators. The first major segment features a panel of four presenters demonstrating their travel rule compliance solutions. Elsa Madrol from CoolBitX presents SignaBridge, a secure off-chain platform for sharing compliance information. Philip Gaspard of 21 Analytics demonstrates a full-stack compliance software that supports multiple protocols including TRP and OpenVASP. Pelle Brangard of Nota Bene showcases a holistic platform for managing transaction compliance and counterparty due diligence. Joseph Weinberg of Shift Network introduces Veriscope, a decentralized identity and discovery layer built on public blockchains. Following the product demonstrations, the discussion shifts to the status and future of the IVMS 101 standard. Sean O’Connor, Malcolm, and representatives from OpenVASP, TRP, and Trisa discuss the rapid development of the standard over 18 weeks and its adoption by major solution providers. They identify key challenges for the next phase, including VASP directory standards, secure data storage, and interoperability between different protocols. The panel emphasizes that while IVMS 101 solves data formatting issues, further work is needed on trust mechanisms and cross-protocol communication. The summit concludes with a summary panel where Sean O’Connor, Mark Pesce, and Bryony reflect on the two days of discussions. They address the growing complexity of regulating DeFi and non-custodial wallets, arguing that current intermediary-focused regulations may not fit a trustless financial ecosystem. They call for a unified industry voice and more frequent dialogue with the FATF. Lawrence provides closing remarks emphasizing the need for proportionate regulation and safe harbor mechanisms. Finally, a representative from the FATF acknowledges the industry’s progress, reiterates the organization’s commitment to continued dialogue, and announces plans for a second 12-month review report in the coming year.
