# The New Inventors-20070411200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their creations to a panel of judges, including ag scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and journalist and inventor Christine Kanimoth. The first invention presented is the Channel Panel by Ian Edmonds from Brisbane, a device made of two sheets of acrylic plastic with angled laser cuts. When hung in a window, the panel redirects sunlight onto the ceiling to illuminate deep parts of office buildings, reducing the need for electric lighting and lowering room temperatures. Edmonds notes that a single panel can provide light equivalent to four fluorescent tubes and has a four-year payback time. The judges praise its design and marketability, with Chris Russell selecting it as the winner of the night, putting Edmonds in the running for the Inventor of the Year award. The second invention is a boat hoist and trailer combination by Steve Scott. Designed for retirees who travel with caravans and boats, the device allows a boat to be stored on the roof of a vehicle and then lowered into the water using guide rails and a winch. The structure can be disassembled and reconfigured into a standard trailer. The judges discuss the complexity of the assembly process, which takes about 45 minutes, and the weight limitations of four-wheel drive vehicles. While they acknowledge the niche market appeal, they note the high cost of nearly 5,000 dollars and the need for industrial design refinement. The final invention is the Easy Chamfer by John Dowell, a tool attached to a hand drill that shaves the edges of pipes to the correct angle. This allows pipes to fit smoothly over rubber seals without damaging them, preventing leaks in water and sewer mains. Dowell demonstrates the tool, which costs 780 dollars and is targeted at commercial plumbers and civil contractors rather than domestic users. The judges appreciate the tool's effectiveness and design but note its significant weight and high price point. The episode concludes with a call for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award and a closing quote from Oscar Wilde.
