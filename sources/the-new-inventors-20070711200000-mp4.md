# The New Inventors-20070711200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features a panel of judges, including agricultural scientist Chris Russell, science journalist Bernie Hobbs, and engineering professor Veena Sahajwala, who evaluate three new inventions. The episode begins with a promotional segment for a controversial documentary on global warming, followed by the main program. The first invention presented is a solid-state LED light by Keith Higgins from the Blue Mountains, New South Wales. Higgins claims his light uses one-fifth the power of conventional lighting, produces virtually no heat, and lasts ten years. He explains that he redesigned the architecture of the wafer to convert nearly all energy into light and integrated the AC to DC rectifier directly into the diodes. Although the device is not patented, Higgins states it has been tested on a highway in China for over two years. The panel is impressed by the potential energy savings but notes the lack of independent Australian testing. The second invention is the Gannett Swing Away Steering Console by Gari Luxford, a pivoting and removable steering wheel designed for small boats to maximize deck space. The third invention is Flexine, a flexible polystyrene cladding material by Andrew Killam, which allows for the construction of curved walls and ceilings at a cost of approximately 45 dollars per square meter. The panel discusses its recyclability, fire safety compliance, and ease of installation. In the final segment, the judges vote for the night's winner. Veena Sahajwala and Chris Russell select the solid-state lighting for its originality and market potential, while Bernie Hobbs favors the Gannett Swing Away for its unique design. Ultimately, the panel unanimously awards the win to Keith Higgins and his solid-state lighting, placing him in the running for the Inventor of the Year award. The episode concludes with a prompt for viewers to vote for the People's Choice Award and a quote from Bob Dylan.
