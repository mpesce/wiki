# newinventors.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The panel of judges includes engineer James Bradfield Moody, interior designer Allison Page, and author and futurist Mark Pesce. The episode features several Australian inventors presenting their designs to the judges for feedback. The first segment introduces Ron Byhelfer from Melbourne, who presents an adjustable air duct system. Byhelfer explains that traditional ducts are difficult to install when obstacles like pipes are in the way, often requiring a return to the workshop for custom fabrication. His invention uses a load-bearing, airtight hinge and extra sheeting to allow the duct to curve around obstacles on-site. The judges discuss the potential for standardizing sizes and the cost savings of eliminating workshop returns, with Mark Pesce praising the obviousness and beauty of the solution. Next, Rod Shepherd from Marysville presents the Eco Switch, a device designed to eliminate standby power consumption. Shepherd notes that standby power accounts for approximately 10 percent of domestic energy use in Australia. The device is a plug-in switch that allows users to completely cut power to a power board, ensuring that "off" truly means off. The judges highlight the simplicity of the design and its potential for international markets, particularly in the United States where wall outlets lack built-in switches. Mark Pesce suggests adding a small fuse to protect delicate equipment while maintaining the device's simplicity. The final segment features Mark Lee from Sydney, accompanied by 2006 Australian Iron Woman champion Naomi Flood, who demonstrates shin fins. Unlike traditional foot flippers, these fins attach to the leg above the ankle using wide rubber straps. This design allows for a more natural swimming kick, reducing strain on the ankles and calves while preventing the fins from falling off in waves. The judges inquire about the grip of the straps and the cost, which is listed at 99 dollars for a pair. The episode also includes brief mentions of historical inventions from Gallipoli, such as the periscope rifle and drip rifles, and a preview of shoes with training wheels.
