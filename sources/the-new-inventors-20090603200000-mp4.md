## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features three inventors presenting their products to a panel of judges: agricultural scientist Chris Russell, interior designer Alison Page, and inventor and journalist Christine Kinenmock. The first invention is the Biopod, a biodegradable shroud burial system presented by funeral director Kevin Hartley. Hartley explains that his design uses a rigid bearer board and carry bars to allow a cloth shroud to be transported in a hearse and lowered into a grave with dignity, avoiding the environmental impact of coffins and embalming fluids. The judges discuss the elegance of the system and its cost, which ranges from $500 to over $1,000, noting that it offers a natural return to the earth.
