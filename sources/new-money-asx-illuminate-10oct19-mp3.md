# NEW MONEY ASX Illuminate 10Oct19.mp3

## Summary

Talk on 'new money' at the ASX Illuminate event (10 Oct 2019). 'Money is changing, and when money changes, it changes everything.' He opens with a story: a very even-keeled friend, 'buzzing at the seams,' recommended an amazing new bank. Mark, incredulous ('Banks are where you keep money, you withdraw money, you get a credit card, you have a mortgage. That's about it'), was told to 'just download the app.' After ~2 minutes of onboarding (100 points of ID) he got a pay ID to receive transfers and a digital debit card issued immediately into his iPhone, set up for Apple Pay — and he transferred $50 and touched his phone to an FPOS terminal and 'it all just worked. I had been waiting three years for my bank to adopt Apple Pay.' The thesis: 'the 21st century is the century of services, just as the 20th century was the century of products, and we are going to need financial instruments that allow us to pay for those services. That's really why we will be adopting digital currencies here.' As we do, 'we're going to be forced to rethink every business model: can it be delivered as a service, how can we bill for it, how do we make it easy to consume on demand, how do we integrate it with others to build value chains?' 'Digital currencies gift us with new capacities, new potentials... a foundation for incredible economic growth. All of this is only going to happen if businesses take advantage of it.'
