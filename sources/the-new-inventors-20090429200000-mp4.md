# The New Inventors-20090429200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features a panel of judges, including engineering professor Veena Sahajwala, agricultural scientist Chris Russell, and designer Ellison Page, who evaluate three distinct inventions. The first inventor, Stuart Elliott, presents Solomate, a solar heating and cooling system for homes. The device uses a hollow panel with a honeycomb structure and triple-glazed design to trap solar energy, heating air that is then circulated through the house. It also functions as a cooling system in summer by drawing in cool nighttime air. Elliott notes the system costs approximately 3,500 dollars per module and reduces energy bills by 50 to 80 percent. The second segment features Barry Bennett from Beruga, New South Wales, who demonstrates a trolley designed to assist sheep shearers. The invention addresses common back and knee injuries caused by dragging heavy sheep to shearing boards. The trolley uses a gentle grip on the sheep's hindquarters to transport the animal safely. Bennett explains that while the trolley adds a few seconds to the shearing process, it significantly improves safety and ergonomics, particularly as sheep breeds have become larger. The panel acknowledges the critical need for occupational health and safety solutions in the agricultural sector. The final invention is a spill guard for paint tins, presented by Terry Randolph from Sydney. The device is a funnel-like attachment that fits over the rim of a paint container, preventing spills when the tin is accidentally knocked over. Randolph demonstrates that the design allows for easy cleaning and extends the pot life of the paint by reducing exposure to air and sun. The judges deliberate on the criteria of originality, need, and market potential. Veena Sahajwala and Chris Russell ultimately select Solomate as the winner, citing its efficient manufacturing process and significant market impact, while acknowledging the cleverness of the spill guard. The episode concludes with a mention of the previous week's winner and upcoming programming on ABC 2.
