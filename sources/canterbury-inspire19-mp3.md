# Canterbury INSPIRE19.mp3

## Summary

Talk at INSPIRE (Canterbury, NZ). He notes his voice may be familiar from appearing with Katherine Ryan on Thursdays, and that he has four podcasts. As a professional futurist ('a lot closer to what a climate scientist does' than a psychic), he takes the audience through 'the next billion seconds' (~30 years, out to 2050) and the THREE LARGEST TRENDS, which break down 'very neatly' into THREE PARTNERSHIPS: (1) the MACHINE-TO-MACHINE partnership, (2) the HUMAN-TO-MACHINE partnership, and (3) the HUMAN-TO-HUMAN partnership — 'really transforming the way we live and work.' He works through them with examples: the future of aeronautics isn't just 'firing rockets at orbit' but 'all the other services and support that people need to keep their eyes on things' (the human-to-machine partnership); and Guardian Circle (advised since 2014), which lets people nominate guardians to be called in an emergency — important in countries without a strong emergency-responder network (no 000, no 911). He closes: 'Machine-to-machine partnerships will completely transform the economy. Human-to-machine partnerships will completely transform how we work. And the human-to-human partnerships will completely transform how we learn.'
