# F2_cold-open.mp4

## Summary

Clip. The 'watershed' concept: using AI in an agentic way was expensive because a human had to be in the loop all the time. Once you cross the watershed, the human is ON the loop, just checking in occasionally, making sure things stay on track — and that changes the calculus of how much cognitive labor costs. That is the core of the watershed: before it, for all of history, cognitive labor had one source — us.
