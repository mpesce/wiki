# The New Inventors-20070509200000.mp4

## Summary

This recording is an episode of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation television program The New Inventors, hosted by James O'Loughlin. The show features a panel of judges, including futurist and author Mark Pesce, engineering professor Venus Sahajwala, and woodworker teacher Richard Vaughan, who evaluate three new inventions. The first invention presented is the Painter's Briefcase by Michael Ware, a foldable paint tray designed to prevent spillage and extend paint life during breaks. Ware explains that the vertical housing allows for easy transport between rooms, and the judges discuss its utility for both professional and amateur painters, noting its simple design and potential cost under sixty dollars. The second invention is the Biter Fighter by Bronwyn Jamison, a pair of foam plugs that seal boot openings to prevent spiders, snakes, and other creatures from entering. Jamison, from a mining town in Western Australia, demonstrates how the foam expands to block entry while a connecting cord keeps the pair together. The panel highlights the serious safety risks of venomous creatures in footwear, particularly in rural and mining areas, and notes the product's low cost of under ten dollars. The third invention is the Fume Tube by Steven Terpstra, an exhaust extension for diesel or petrol-powered cutting tools that uses a wet vacuum cleaner to pump dangerous fumes, such as carbon monoxide, out of enclosed workspaces. Terpstra explains that the system mixes water with the exhaust to cool it and prevent damage to the hose, and he notes that approximately two hundred units have been sold so far. In the final segment, the judges deliberate on the winner based on originality, need, and commercial potential. Mark Pesce praises the elegance of all three designs but ultimately favors the Fume Tube due to the widespread global risk of carbon monoxide poisoning and the large number of workers affected. The other judges agree, citing safety and market potential, leading to a unanimous decision in favor of Steven Terpstra's Fume Tube. The episode concludes with a call for audience voting for the Inventors Choice Award and a closing quote from Douglas Adams.
