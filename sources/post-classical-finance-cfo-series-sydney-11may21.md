# Post-Classical Finance CFO Series Sydney 11May21.mp4

## Summary

This recording is a presentation by Mark Pesce, a professional futurist and host of the podcast The Next Billion Seconds, discussing the evolution of money and digital assets. The talk begins with the 2008 release of the Bitcoin whitepaper by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto, which introduced a method for wrapping data to make it authenticatable, auditable, and unique. This technology, known as blockchain, allows for consensus-based verification of data integrity. Pesce explains that this foundation enabled the creation of Bitcoin, a digital currency that cannot be counterfeited, and subsequently led to the development of smart contracts. These are computer programs wrapped in blockchain technology that can operate on digital currencies, allowing for automated and verifiable financial transactions. The speaker contrasts classical payment systems, which rely on bank infrastructure and delayed settlement, with post-classical digital currencies stored in smartphone wallets. He highlights the global push for central bank digital currencies, citing the Chinese Digital Yuan, the European Central Bank’s digital euro project, and the Reserve Bank of Australia’s pilot programs. These initiatives aim to provide resilient, immediate settlement systems and address the needs of the three billion unbanked people worldwide who possess smartphones but lack access to traditional banking. Pesce also discusses private alternatives like Facebook’s Diem and the rise of decentralized finance, where smart contracts allow individuals to manage investments and loans directly on their devices. The presentation concludes with an exploration of non-fungible tokens, which transform digital works such as music, art, and even newspaper columns into unique, tradable assets. Pesce predicts a future where all data and physical items are wrapped in blockchain, becoming financial instruments that can be insured, securitized, and traded. He envisions a late 2020s landscape of financialization where code and value are inseparable, creating an ecosystem of active assets that continuously seek to maximize their worth through smart contracts. The core message is that understanding this fusion of code and value is essential for thriving in the era of post-classical finance.
