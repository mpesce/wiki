# Monash

## TL;DR

The University of Melbourne's neighbor — venue of the 2016 data-visualization lecture and home of podcast host Dr Susan Carlin.

## Overview

Monash (Monash University) holds two roles in the corpus. First, **the venue**: the 2016 data-visualization lecture (Monash.wav) was delivered to "an audience of university educators and executives, likely at Monash University in Australia" — the talk's core argument is that data visualization improves communication, decision-making and survey participation: text is cognitively expensive, "humans are biologically wired to absorb visual patterns thousands of times faster than text", the Space Shuttle Columbia disaster's poor data presentation is the cautionary example, the Virtual NYSE and the City of Sydney IT dashboards are the positive ones, and the strategic framework is lean methodology (start small, embed measurement, iterate) ([[sources/monash-wav]]). Second, **the affiliation**: in the 2020 Next Billion Seconds podcast (the best current affairs awards, supported by Spotify), the nominees include "What Happens Next, which is hosted by **Dr. Susan Carlin of Monash University**"; the awards went Bronze to 7am, Silver to Full Story, Gold to Background Briefing (ABC) ([[sources/pesce-best-current-affairs-podcast-mp4]]). The corpus gives no other Monash detail — no faculty, no specific building, no named staff beyond Carlin.
