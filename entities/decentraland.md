# Decentraland

## TL;DR

The third wave of virtual worlds — after VRML and Second Life — that in the corpus is both the model of decentralization and the cautionary tale of speculative virtual real estate.

## Overview

Decentraland appears in the corpus in the April 2021 Uptown radio interviews (two recordings of the same conversation) and the "A House Built on Sand" interoperability talk. The stable account: the **waves** run VRML-based worlds (mid-1990s) → Second Life (early 2000s) → **Decentraland (third wave)**; Decentraland's **decentralized, peer-to-peer** architecture is contrasted with Second Life's centralized server — the former offers "greater resilience because data is distributed across users' computers rather than dependent on a single authoritative source" ([[sources/uptown-radio-recording-1-2021-04-26-t12-31-18am]]). Pesce **critiques the economic foundation**: the MANA token is a "**Tinkerbell operation**" — "value is created through collective belief rather than intrinsic utility" — and the "Landrush" events compare to "casino dynamics"; the platform is "fundamentally capitalist and subject to speculative market forces" ([[sources/uptown-radio-recording-1-2021-04-26-t12-31-18am]]). The second recording adds the **Atari influx** — "a virtual strip mall where commerce eventually overtakes the initial artistic community" — and the technical base: "Decentraland and its currency, Mana, are built on the **Ethereum** blockchain"; the journalist is researching a seven-minute audio story on Decentraland, and Pesce is "skeptical" of the platform's long-term relevance beyond the current hype cycle ([[sources/uptown-radio-recording-1-2021-04-26-t12-31-18am-2]]). In the interoperability talk, Decentraland is one of the platforms (with The Sandbox, Cryptovoxels and Meta's Horizon Worlds) that "claim to be the metaverse, but none are interoperable with one another" — the virtual real-estate deeds are "superficial and lack foundational stability", redeemable only within their specific platforms ([[sources/a-house-built-on-sand-interoperability-in-the-me]]). The corpus's Decentraland is the 2021 hype-cycle platform: the model of decentralization Pesce admires architecturally, and the speculative market he distrusts.
