# James Bradfield Moody

## TL;DR

The engineer judge of *The New Inventors* — also a frequent co-host, and the panel's one self-made inventor: author of the Jam Bot software that improvises musical accompaniment.

## Overview

James Bradfield Moody sits on the *New Inventors* panel as its "engineer," and he is the judge the corpus most often promotes to the host's chair — the 2009 stereo-guitar episode, the 2007 fence-tester episode, and the 2009 racing-safety episode all open with him hosting ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20071017200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20090211200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20091028200000-mp4]]). His votes skew technical: the GEM expansion-joint system, the spring-valve shock absorber, the Phase Changer single-to-three-phase converter, the GasTech diesel injection retrofit, the Whale Shark photo-ID library (with Pesce), and the Live Lens spatial-aperture camera chip ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20070314195900-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20071121200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070829200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20090211200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070815200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20091118200000-mp4]]).

The unusual fact in the corpus is that Moody is himself an inventor on the show. In the November 2009 episode hosted by Mark Pesce, he presents his own invention — **Jam Bot**, software that listens to live music and improvises accompaniment by extracting tempo and bar structure, then generates a rhythmic response (initially percussion, with melodic detection planned) — and the other judges evaluate it like anyone else's ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20091104200000-mp4]]). That a panel judge pitched to his own panel is a small but concrete detail about the show's culture: invention was the house value, and the judges' expertise was expected to be demonstrable, not merely titular. Moody also judged the 2007 and 2008 grand finals' feeder episodes and anchored the 2009 disaster-technology special with Hobbs and Pesce ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20071120200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20090610200000-mp4]]).

## Debates / Open Questions


- **Judge-and-inventor conflict, benign in practice.** Moody presented Jam Bot to a panel that included the show's regular judges; the episode records no recusals or special handling. Whether ABC's format ever codified this is outside the corpus.
- **Name variants.** The ASR renders him variously as "James Bradfield Moody," "James Bradford Moody," and "James Bradford Moody" — the ingest folds these to one slug; the Jam Bot episode confirms the engineer judge is the same person who hosted.
