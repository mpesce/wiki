# Wired

## TL;DR

The magazine appears in the corpus as a cited authority — the article that broke Cherub and the article behind "dense time" — with two of its six tags being the verb "wired" misread as the noun.

## Overview

Wired (the magazine) is a small but real presence in the corpus, and the entity page must be read with the caveat that two of its six tagged sources are **false positives** on the verb. The genuine appearances: (1) In the **Hyperdistribution** talk, the community theater group's satirical show **Cherub** "gained attention through a Wired article and iTunes podcasting" — the magazine is the medium that broke the new landscape in which "anyone can reach an audience without traditional industry backing" ([[sources/4-02-hyperdistribution-mp3]]). (2) In the **Pandemic Time** lecture (ANU 3AI, 15 May 2020, two recordings), Pesce defines "dense time" — a period where exponential change makes the forward planning horizon contract to ~30 minutes — and "cites a Wired article" for the claim that when exponential growth peaks, "the horizon bends into a wall, rendering standard predictive models useless" ([[sources/pandemic-time-3ai-15may20-wav]], [[sources/pesce-pandemic-time-anu-3ai-15may20-mp4]]). (3) In the **Knew Money** talk (24 September 2019) the phrase "wired money" appears in the crypto crash-course, likely his coinage for electronic money rather than the magazine ([[sources/knew-money-24sep19-mp3]]). The two verb tags: "Wired into a building's fire alarm system" (the Fire Exit Paver, *New Inventors* 2009) and "humans are biologically wired to absorb visual patterns" (Monash talk, 2016) — both are the verb, not the magazine ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20090304200000-mp4]], [[sources/monash-wav]]). The corpus gives no issue date, author, or title for any of the Wired references — they are cited as bare authorities.
