# Sally Deminks

## TL;DR

The designer-inventor on *The New Inventors* panel from 2007 — a judge, a grand-final decider, and the show's self-described "inventor and designer" voice.

## Overview

Sally Deminks (ASR variants: Deminks, Deminks, Domingues) is introduced on the *New Inventors* panel as an "inventor and designer" or "designer", and she is among the most consistent judges in the corpus — sitting in from the 2007 season through the 2009 grand final ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20070221195900-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20091125200000-mp4]]). Her credits include the two grand finals the corpus contains (2007 with Russell and Hobbs, 2009 with Russell and Hobbs), the 2007 water special, and the 2007 Bright Sparks young-inventor episode ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20071128200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20091125200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070530200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070704200000-mp4]]).

Her individual votes lean toward market clarity and low-tech elegance: she initially backed the Aqua Genie fish-tank cleaner on "clever application of existing reverse-flushing technology" before converging with the panel on the Conehead helmet, questioned the hygiene of the $42,000 catering unit's assembly clips while suggesting waste-heat recovery for the cooling unit, and in the 2009 grand final was one of the three judges who named Colin Chamberlain's safety propeller the 2009 Invention of the Year ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20070822200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20090520200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20091125200000-mp4]]). On the 2009 finger-saving hinge episode she was the design judge alongside Pesce and Wood, and the panel named David Ashard's hinge the year's Inventor of the Year ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20090923200000-mp4]]).
