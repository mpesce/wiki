# True Hallucinations

## TL;DR

Pesce's 2003 psychedelic music project — three songs (Radiance, Strophariad, The Incident) in a dozen format variants, the corpus's only non-verbal work.

## Overview

True Hallucinations is a psychedelic audio project by Mark Pesce, represented in the corpus by twelve files that are the same three musical pieces in different formats. The pieces: **Radiance** (an upbeat vocal piece — "When we depart, when we leave the room / only then can our presence be felt / love is the scent that lingers in the air", with the refrain "Radiance / glorious, impermanent" and "Night clears the sky and we become the stars"); **Strophariad** (an instrumental with sung/chant passages — "They have grown, by some miracle unknown... my perfect stone, a tomb of stone... I believe in the beauty of the world... a unity of hope"); and **The Incident** (a dramatic instrumental with field sounds of crickets and birds) ([[sources/radiance-mobile-mp4]], [[sources/strophariad-rm]], [[sources/the-incident-mp4]]). The only dated artifact is the "Radiance" vocal take of 5 April 2003 ([[sources/radiancemarkvocal050403-mp3]]).

The format spread is the corpus's real content: 384 kHz RealAudio master (.rm), mobile video exports, and iPod exports with burned-in subtitles — a small artist distribution matrix predating streaming, with the same piece rendered for each device ([[sources/radiance384-rm]], [[sources/strophariad384-rm]], [[sources/incident384-rm]], [[sources/strophariad-ipod-subtitles-mp4]], [[sources/the-incident-ipod-subtitles-mp4]]). One clip, erowid.mp4, is a short spoken piece in an Erowid (psychedelic-drug reference) context, anchoring the project's genre ([[sources/erowid-mp4]]). No source discusses the project's production, release, or reception — the corpus holds the work itself, nothing about it. The project sits on the same axis as the Esalen weekend's psychedelic material (the mushroom, the "unity of hope") but is never linked to it in any source.
