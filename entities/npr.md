# NPR

## TL;DR

The broadcaster of the PBS/NPR documentary "Merchants of Cool" — in the corpus, a single reference from the 2001 Esalen weekend.

## Overview

NPR (National Public Radio) appears in the corpus in exactly one reference: the **Saturday Afternoon** session of the 2001 Esalen "Trilogues for a New Millennium" weekend, where the conversation (Erik Davis reading Philip K. Dick's "The Electric Ant", then the discussion of agency) turns to "the subversive PBS/NPR documentary **'Merchants of Cool'**" — the argument being "how corporations set the agenda and create the 'culture of cool' and feed it back to kids before they can form their own" ([[sources/saturday-afternoon-rm]]). The corpus's NPR is the co-broadcaster (with PBS) of that documentary — the institution behind the "culture of cool" critique that the Esalen panel treats as evidence of corporate agenda-setting in children's media.
