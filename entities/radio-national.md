# Radio National

## TL;DR

ABC Radio National, the radio home of a string of Mark Pesce interviews across 2006–2017 — The Book Show, The Drum, Common Knowledge and Life Matters.

## Overview

Radio National (ABC Radio National) appears in the corpus as the outlet for several Pesce interviews and one news segment. The **Book Show** (Romana Koval, 23 June 2006): a panel of experts, including Pesce, on storytelling and writing in computer games — "more and more games are exploring sophisticated narrative elements as film-savvy players expect more in terms of character development and storyline"; "choice is the new buzzword"; cutscenes as the plot vehicle ([[sources/4-03-the-book-show-23-june-2006-mp3]]). **The Drum** (2011, in the context of the early WikiLeaks controversy): Pesce on the fragility of trust, information warfare, and the Microsoft Kinect as the next standard ([[sources/the-drum-youtube-1-m4v]]). **The dot-com bubble check** (14 May 2011): three experts — Jody Rich (People Browser CEO), Violet Ho (Kroll Consulting, Beijing), and Pesce — on whether a bubble is forming; Rich says no (a Darwinian process), Pesce describes "frothy exuberance" but no rush to go public, Ho gives the China regulatory angle ([[sources/sea-20110514-0805-1-mp3]]). **Common Knowledge** (19 February 2012): two segments — Pesce on **Anonymous** and the Greek financial crisis (DDoS attacks on government sites, the leaderless horizontal collective, the PasteBin threat to DNS) and Edwina McCann on the red carpet as advertising real estate ([[sources/rn-common-knowledge-19feb12-mp3]]). **Life Matters** (5 May 2017): location tracking and family privacy — Joanne Orlando (UWS researcher) on parental surveillance apps, Pesce on GPS/Android/Apple privacy settings and the argument that continuous tracking amplifies parental anxiety ([[sources/lms-20170505-0906-families-privacy-mp3]]). The sixth source, the 2007-11-14 *New Inventors* file, is described in the corpus as an Australian news broadcast (2007 federal election coverage, Michael Brissenden, Heather Hewitt, an Ian Rankin interview) "likely ABC Radio National" — the only source where the RN attribution is inferred rather than stated ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20071114200000-mp4]]). The corpus's Radio National is the Australian broadcast home where Pesce is the recurring futurist voice, and the outlet's programs are the containers: The Book Show, The Drum, Common Knowledge, Life Matters.
