# Christine Kininmonth

## TL;DR

The "inventor and journalist" who rotates the *New Inventors* panel — in the corpus, a two-episode judge with no off-show identity.

## Overview

Christine Kininmonth (ASR variants: Christine Kenimont, Christine Kinenmonth) is one of the rotating judges on *The New Inventors*, credited in the 2009-06-17 episode as **"inventor and journalist"** — the only judge in the corpus whose on-screen title combines invention and journalism ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20090617200000-mp4]]). In the corpus she appears on two episodes: the 2009-06-17 episode (with Chris Russell and Fiona Wood), where the panel selects the **Height Guard** scaffolding safety barrier ("the judges praise its originality and safety benefits, noting it is quicker and safer than traditional harnesses") ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20090617200000-mp4]]); and the 2009-10-14 episode (with James Bradfield Moody and Richard Vaughan), where the panel selects the **wool bale fastener gun** ("the judges praise the practicality of the solution for the wool industry, noting it is an early prototype that requires further refinement in ergonomics and loading mechanisms") ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20091014200000-mp4]]). As with the other judges, the corpus gives her no identity beyond the chair — no publication, no invention named, no off-show interview; the "inventor and journalist" credit is the one biographical fact it carries.
