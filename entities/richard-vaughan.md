# Richard Vaughan

## TL;DR

The woodworker on *The New Inventors* panel — maker-credibility in a show built for makers.

## Overview

Richard Vaughan appears in the corpus as the "woodworker" (sometimes "woodworking teacher") who rotates through the *New Inventors* judging panel, usually alongside Russell, Hobbs, Page, or Moody ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20070328195900-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070725200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20080924200000-mp4]]). His votes track the panel's practical axis: he backs the FAS foot-alignment system with Hobbs on practical need and accuracy, the Smart Console's elegance and inventiveness, the Aztecs tactile-measurement tool (with Russell, citing its potential as a standard diagnostic for nerve-injury and diabetes patients), and the horse-bit tongue plate's clear market demand ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20070328195900-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070808200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20080924200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20090225200000-mp4]]).

The 2009 episodes show him in the show's later, more technical register: with Moody and Kininmonth he judges the Live Lens spatial-aperture camera chip (Moody's pick, on market potential) and the Drain Wave sewer-surge device, and he is one of the judges in the wool-bale fastener gun and safety-propeller episodes that feed the 2009 grand final ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20091118200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20091014200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20091111200000-mp4]]). His woodworker's title makes him the panel's only maker-credibility seat — the show's closest analogue to a fellow practitioner judging other practitioners' work.

## Debates / Open Questions


- **The maker-judge question.** Vaughan's craft title (woodworker) is the corpus's only instance of a panel seat defined by hands-on making rather than science, journalism, or design. Whether ABC deliberately kept a maker on the panel is unrecorded, but the pattern (17 episodes, 2007–2009) suggests it was a standing role.
