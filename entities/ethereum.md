# Ethereum

## TL;DR

The blockchain on which, in the corpus, the practical financial future is built — DeFi smart contracts, the RBA's wholesale currency, and Decentraland's MANA.

## Overview

Ethereum enters the corpus as the blockchain base for the three concrete financial applications Pesce cites in the 2020–2021 financial talks. (1) **DeFi**: in the Queensland CFO talk (10 September 2020), "the rise of decentralized finance, specifically **Ethereum-based smart contracts**, which are creating over-collateralized loan pools worth billions" — the technology forecast to "disintermediate traditional business banking" ([[sources/beyond-the-horizon-cfo-series-qld-10sep20-mp4]]). (2) **The RBA wholesale currency**: in the Melbourne CFO talk (12 November 2020), the Reserve Bank of Australia announced "to create a wholesale digital currency using **Ethereum smart contracts** to modernize bank settlements" — the Australian central bank adopting the platform for interbank settlement ([[sources/cfo-series-melbourne-while-you-were-out-12nov20]]). (3) **Decentraland/MANA**: in the Uptown radio interview (26 April 2021), "Decentraland and its currency, Mana, are built on the **Ethereum** blockchain" — the virtual-world base ([[sources/uptown-radio-recording-1-2021-04-26-t12-31-18am-2]]). The corpus also places Ethereum in the **PayPal crypto portfolio** (alongside Bitcoin, one of the four cryptocurrencies PayPal would let users buy and pay with, creating "a regulated, walled garden for crypto transactions") ([[sources/cfo-series-melbourne-while-you-were-out-12nov20]]). Ethereum is the corpus's standing answer to "which blockchain is actually doing work": DeFi loan pools, the RBA, and Decentraland all run on it, while Bitcoin is held as the 2008 origin and Libra as the 2019 flashpoint.

## Debates / Open Questions


- **Ethereum vs Bitcoin in the corpus.** The corpus holds Ethereum as the workhorse (DeFi, RBA, Decentraland) and Bitcoin as the origin (2008 whitepaper). The two are not in tension in the corpus; they occupy different roles in the same financial narrative.
- **The RBA adoption: pilot or commitment?** The corpus describes the RBA announcement as "using Ethereum smart contracts" but does not say whether this is a pilot, a committed system, or a later reversal. The page holds the 2020 announcement as stated.
