# Bernie Hobbs

## TL;DR

The science journalist on *The New Inventors* panel — the practical voice of the broadcast, and a co-host on several episodes.

## Overview

Bernie Hobbs is the corpus's "science journalist" judge on *The New Inventors*, a fixture of the rotating panel from the 2007 season through the 2009 season ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20070228195900-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070523200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20091028200000-mp4]]). His votes read as a journalist's: the invention with the clearest story and the widest audience — the Safe Break electric-fence gate insulator, the Water Fresh sewer-reuse system (winning against the Quick Lock on scalability), the Gannett swing-away steering console, the shark-claw tagging tool (praised for its "originality and beautiful design"), and the commercial freezer defroster (voted for its "global market impact") ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20070314195900-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070523200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070711200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20090805200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20090909200000-mp4]]).

Beyond judging, Hobbs took hosting turns on the show — the 2007 water-themed week opened with "journalists Bernie Hobbs and engineer James Bradfield Moody" co-hosting, and the People's Choice results, a recurring segment he often presented, gave his newsroom role a visible slot in every episode ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20070523200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20070718200000-mp4]]). He judged the 2007 and 2009 grand finals (Conehead and the safety propeller, respectively) and anchored the 2009 post-disaster episode covering bushfire architecture, ANUGA flood modelling, and UNSW micro aerial vehicles ([[sources/the-new-inventors-20071128200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20091125200000-mp4]], [[sources/the-new-inventors-20090610200000-mp4]]).

- **The journalist's bias is the format's bias.** Hobbs's votes consistently reward public-facing, safety-critical inventions with mass markets — the same criteria the show announced to its TV audience. The corpus can't separate his personal judgment from the panel's house style; the two are indistinguishable on these pages.
