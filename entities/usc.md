# USC

## TL;DR

The University of Southern California — in the corpus, one of the two institutions (with AFTRS) where Pesce founded postgraduate programs.

## Overview

USC (the University of Southern California) appears in the corpus in a single reference: the **Smarter Money** talk (19 October 2016, to banking professionals), where Pesce outlines his background — "his invention of a 3D web interface, his role in **founding postgraduate programs at USC and AFTRS**, and his work as a futurist for the ABC and Swift" ([[sources/smarter-money-19oct16-mp3]]). The talk's main argument is the disconnect between SMEs and banking systems in developing nations (the three G20 Antalya fintech solutions — First Access, CopoCopo, Verde International), the history of money (ancient coins, Song Dynasty paper currency, European bills of exchange, the IBM System 360), the blockchain mechanics (taxi receipts and cryptographic hashes), the real-world applications (Full Profile grain payments, Power Ledger energy trading), the IoT security risks (the Brian Krebs attack), the smart contracts and DAO incident ($50M theft, the blockchain fork), and the advice (banks should acquire fintech startups; dual degrees in law and computer science) ([[sources/smarter-money-19oct16-mp3]]). The corpus's USC is Pesce's postgraduate-program founding affiliation; the page holds the USC/AFTRS pairing and the futurist-for-ABC-and-Swift credit as the corpus's USC content.
