# ANU

## TL;DR

The Australian National University, which in the corpus is the employer of Eleanor Huntington (Dean of Engineering), mentioned in the 2019 "Wave of Automation" talk.

## Overview

ANU (the Australian National University) appears in the corpus in a single reference: the **Wave of Automation** talk (2 August 2019, to educators), where Pesce "mentions his friend **Eleanor Huntington (Dean of Engineering, ANU)** is an alum" ([[sources/wave-of-automation-2aug19-mp3]]). The talk's core argument is the wave of automation and its effect on education: the Blockbuster/Netflix anecdote (Blockbuster passed on buying Netflix when it cost $30M, now worth $150B), the last Tower Records (Shinjuku, "a whole building"), the 2004 Melbourne primary-school educators' reaction to Wikipedia ("two years later everyone's made their peace with it"), and the claim that the late-19th/early-20th-century educational systems "were established to create a broad class of workers educated once for an industrial culture that raised millions out of subsistence living into middle-class prosperity. And it totally worked" — the 21st-century system is "revising and reorganizing around a different sort of social contract — one that promises a net of relationality, of mentoring, that will bind us together" ([[sources/wave-of-automation-2aug19-mp3]]). The corpus's ANU is Eleanor Huntington's institution; the page holds the affiliation and the alum detail, and notes that the talk's own venue is not stated in the corpus as ANU.
